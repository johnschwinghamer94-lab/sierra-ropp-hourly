## Coaching

**The missed moment:** Customer said, "One room's always colder. I don't know if you have any way to adjust that."

This is a clear pain point and a flip opportunity. The tech's response—"we'll look at everything"—was passive.

**What should have happened:**
1. **Reconnect to the concern:** "I hear you—that's a common challenge with multi-zone systems. Let me show you a couple of options to balance that temperature without raising your overall cooling costs."
2. **Explain the mechanism:** "Your thermostats control the flow to each zone independently. We can adjust dampers, balance ductwork, or add a smart controller to maintain your preferred temperature in that room."
3. **Present options:** 
   - Option A: Ductwork rebalancing (cost/benefit for seasonal comfort).
   - Option B: Zone damper upgrade for automated control (higher comfort, minimal ongoing cost).
   - Option C: Mini-split in the cold room (if zoning isn't sufficient).
4. **Close:** "Which of these sounds best for your situation?"

**Reinforce:** A 279-minute call is your chance to build comprehensive understanding and earn a significant upgrade. When a customer identifies a concern, that's your signal to reconnect and present options—not to defer.
