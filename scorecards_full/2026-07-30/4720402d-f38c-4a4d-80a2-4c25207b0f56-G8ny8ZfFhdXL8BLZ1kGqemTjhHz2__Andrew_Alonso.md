## Coaching Notes

**What earned this flip:**  
This call flipped because the tech used **technical explanation as a trust-builder**. When the customer asked about maintenance timing, the tech didn't say "Let me upsell you a premium service." Instead: "Let's test with a fresh motor so we get accurate readings." The customer bought not because of pressure, but because the reasoning made sense. That's the move to repeat.

**Specific reinforcement:**
The line "When I test the power of the motor, it'll be the power of the new motor, not the old one" is the core of why this customer went from "maybe I'll do maintenance" to "yes, do it after the motor." Tech made the customer feel smart about the decision.

**To strengthen next calls:**
1. **Delivery depth:** When explaining what maintenance includes, move from list mode ("we test everything") to diagnostic mode ("here's what we found last time on a similar system and why it matters").
2. **Midpoint check-ins:** Add a "Does this plan feel right for you?" checkpoint midway through the decision section, even if no objection has surfaced. It invites the customer to voice concerns early.
3. **Close:** Add an explicit ask for the 5-star review at the end, even if payment is already done.
