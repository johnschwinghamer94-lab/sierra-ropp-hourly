## Coaching Tip

**FIX** — When the customer said "I'm glad it's something simple," that was your opening to build value. At that moment, present upgrade options: "We can replace just the damper for around $450, or if you want to optimize your whole zoning system we could do a ductwork balance check for $150 more to prevent this from happening again. Which would work better for you?" Then directly ask for the sale instead of just deferring to ordering the part. The customer was relieved—they were ready to buy something.
