## Outcome: **No-Close**

## Summary
Joseph delivered an exemplary diagnostic and options presentation. His technical depth was evident, his communication was clear, and he built credibility through detailed findings and plain-language explanations. The weak point: when the customer expressed financial constraint, Joseph had an opportunity to reframe the value (compressor failure would cost $3000+; the $1,351 soft start protects a $3000 compressor and saves energy) or explore payment flexibility. Instead, he accepted the deferral gracefully but missed the close.

---

## Coaching Tip
**FIX — When the customer said 'We're on fixed income, so we'll think about it,' you could have said, 'I understand—but here's the reality: if that compressor fails, you're looking at a $3,000 replacement. This $1,351 soft start actually pays for itself in energy savings plus protects your compressor. Let's see if a payment plan makes it work today.' That reframe—connecting the price to what failure costs—would likely have closed the sale."**

3. **Urgency & Value Tie:** Didn't link the cost of inaction to the price of the solution.
