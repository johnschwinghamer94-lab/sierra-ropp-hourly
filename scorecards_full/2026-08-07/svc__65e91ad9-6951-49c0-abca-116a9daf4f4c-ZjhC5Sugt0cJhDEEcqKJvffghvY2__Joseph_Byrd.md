## Coaching Tip

**FIX** — When the customer said "We're on fixed income, so we'll think about it," that was your moment to empathize ("I understand, that's a constraint") then ask: "Would a monthly payment plan make this work, or is there a lower-cost partial solution we could explore?" Instead, you moved to financing (which they couldn't qualify for) and let them walk. The objection was not about lack of interest in the fix—it was about affordability. Address that directly.
