## Gaps & Coaching

**Primary Gap:** Expectations missing at the gate.  
23 minutes is enough time to diagnose AND present options, but only if you set expectations at the start. Open every call: *"Hi, here's how this works: I'll check your outdoor unit, filters, and ductwork—takes about 20 minutes. If I find something, I'll show you what it is and walk through your options before we commit to anything. Sound good?"* This frames the entire visit and builds trust.

**Secondary Gap:** Diagnosis rushed or incomplete.  
Even if you're short on time, don't leave without findings. If you haven't completed diagnosis by 15 minutes in, tell the customer: *"I need another 10 minutes to finish checking the indoor coil, but I'm already seeing [one thing]. Let me grab the rest of the picture and I'll have options for you."* Transparency keeps them engaged.
