## Gaps & Coaching

**Primary Gap:** Missing expectations framework.  
When a customer calls with a problem, set the stage early: *"I'll spend about 15 minutes checking your outdoor unit, filters, and ductwork. The inspection itself is free—if I find something, I'll walk you through what happened and your options before we proceed. Any questions?"* This takes 30 seconds and eliminates surprise.

**Secondary Gap:** No options ready.  
Even if diagnosis takes the full call, always exit with 3–4 price points. *"It looks like a failed capacitor. I can replace just the cap for $X, or we can add a soft-start protector for $Y to prevent this next time, or we could replace the whole contactor for full protection at $Z."* Different budgets, different risk tolerances—give customers a choice.
