## Coaching Notes

### TOP WIN: Negotiation + empathy = sale in crisis
**The moment it worked**:
- Customer initially frustrated: "I don't [want to] pay five hundred [dollars]"
- Tech acknowledged: "Yeah, yeah. I need a price for the stuff."
- Customer opened door for negotiation: "Obviously we don't want to lowball you guys... Just give us the lowest you can do."
- Tech responded: "I'll call my boss right now... because you got like 4 kids, a few grandparents in here... I got $100 off."
- Customer: "Hey, appreciate it."

**What made it work**:
1. Tech didn't just say "that's the price" (would have lost sale)
2. Tech didn't apologize for the price (appropriate - it was fair)
3. Tech put in effort in real-time (called boss while customer watched)
4. Tech tied discount to family situation (showed empathy, not just discounting)
5. Customer felt heard and appreciated the effort

### Secondary strength: Technical clarity
Tech explained complex issues (ECM fan, MERV ratings, airflow) in plain language without being condescending. Customer understood the "why" behind recommendations.

### Gap: Membership/SAM opportunity
Customer already had SAM (getting $16/month bill). Tech could have said: "Since you're on SAM, the fan would be covered faster through the priority queue. Let me make sure dispatch knows this is a member — that usually gets expedited." Didn't explicitly reinforce value.
