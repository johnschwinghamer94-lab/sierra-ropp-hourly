## Key Moment

When customer confirmed "yes" to regular maintenance, that was an opening to discuss maintenance packages or warranty options as a value add. Instead, Juan shifted directly to logistics (gate, knock procedure). This was a missed reconnection moment to align on proactive service value.

## Coaching

Juan built good rapport and discovered a maintenance customer, but missed the upsell window. When a customer confirms existing maintenance behavior, position the conversation to explore how we can optimize their plan or add value through options for potential issues.
