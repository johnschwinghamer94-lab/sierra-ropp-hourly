## Gaps & Coaching

**Primary Gap:** Options not tied to the visit.  
Diagnose and present in the same call. After testing, say: *"Here's what I found: [issue]. The right fix depends on your budget. I have three options: [good option @ $X], [better @ $Y], or [best @ $Z]. Which direction works for you?"* Leaving with a promise of options later loses momentum and control of the sale.

**Secondary Gap:** No close or call-to-action.  
Even with pending options, end with: *"I'll send you those three options by end of day. Does Thursday work for an install, or do you prefer Friday?"* Get a tentative commitment.
