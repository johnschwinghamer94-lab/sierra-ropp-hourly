## Coaching Tip

**FIX** — Set clear expectations upfront before you start troubleshooting. When the customer described their two-unit system and the problem, you should have said something like: "I'll take a look at the outdoor unit first, check pressures and electrical connections. That diagnosis takes about 20 minutes, and we charge $79 for the service call. Then I'll come back in and show you what we found." Instead, customers often don't know how long they'll wait or what to expect cost-wise until you're already deep in the work.
