## Top 2 Gaps & Coaching

### Gap 1: No Discovery During Service (Critical)
**The Moment:**  
Beverly mentioned: "I'm surprised you had to park so soon. I was happy about that."  
Josue: "Thankfully, they were able to get it pretty fast."

**What Opened the Door:**  
Customer's positive sentiment about parts availability and quick service was a natural bridge to explore other system needs. A SILO tech could have said: *"Great! While I'm here working on this, are there any other comfort issues or concerns you've had with the system? Any rooms running hot or cold, or other units that need attention?"*

**Why This Matters:**  
Service visits are prime upsell moments. The customer is already invested (parts are here, tech is on-site for an hour+) and satisfied (quick parts turnaround). This is when discovery questions convert best into TGLs.

**The Fix:**  
During the pre-work conversation, ask one consultative question: *"Besides the motor on this unit, are there any other comfort or efficiency concerns in the home?"* This plants the seed for later option creation and Comfort Advisor appointments.

---

### Gap 2: No Option Creation or Value Addition
**The Miss:**  
Josue confirmed the pre-sold work and proceeded directly to execution. He never attempted to:
- Explore additional needs (preventive maintenance, other units, efficiency upgrades)
- Suggest add-on services (system check, ductwork cleaning, thermostat upgrade)
- Schedule a follow-up Comfort Advisor consultation

**What a SILO Tech Should Do:**  
After getting the signature and before starting work, a check-in like this builds trust and opens the door:  
*"While I've got the panel open, I'll do a quick system check for you. That way, if I spot anything that might need attention down the road, we can talk about it after the replacement."*

This reframes the service visit as a consultative moment, not just a transaction.
