## Coaching note

Strong maintenance inspection — customer left satisfied ("system's working great"). The miss: **position protective upgrades earlier.** When you find voltage switch wear ("starting to get some use"), that's your hook to frame soft start as protection, not optional upgrade. Try: "I'm seeing normal wear on the voltage switch. A soft start would reduce strain on that component and extend your system life. Let me walk you through how it works." Tie the feature to the finding, not just features. That moves soft start from "nice-to-have" to "good insurance."
