## Top Gaps & Coaching

**Gap 1: No explicit "next step" timeline** — Bradley says "just give me a couple minutes" but doesn't confirm when the manager call will happen or when the customer should expect to hear back. A sentence like "I'll call the manager right now, then call your husband within the next 10 minutes" would lock in urgency.

**Gap 2: No "be a nerd" summary** — Bradley diagnoses "a couple things... in there" and "outside" but never specifically states what's wrong with the system. The customer doesn't fully understand the problem, which weakens buy-in. He could say: "The compressor's not pumping refrigerant through the coils, which is why you're getting air but no cooling. The manager will explain the full scope when we get him on the line."
