## Coaching Advice

Robert showed strong diagnosis and technical communication. His pressure checks and explanation of the frozen coil were clear and credible. The core coaching moment is in the decision phase: when a customer clearly states a preference (warranty coverage), validate that choice first ("That makes sense—they cover you and it's convenient"), then leave the door open ("If you decide you want a second opinion down the road, give us a call"). This builds trust and often results in customers coming back for the second opinion anyway—especially if the warranty doesn't cover the full replacement cost later.

**Next focus:** When you hear a customer objection, lead with empathy and acknowledgment before pivoting. The phrase "I hear you—that's smart because..." opens more doors than "So you'd rather not...?"
