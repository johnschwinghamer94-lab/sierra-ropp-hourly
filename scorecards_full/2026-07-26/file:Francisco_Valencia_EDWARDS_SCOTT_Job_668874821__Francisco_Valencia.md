## Key Gaps

### Gap 1: No Financing Options Proactively Discussed Until Customer Asked
Francisco discussed payment programs late in the call only after customer asked. For an 87-minute call where replacement costs are 5-10x repair costs, opening with financing options (payment plans, 0% interest, term lengths) earlier would have reduced customer hesitation.

### Gap 2: Limited Discussion of R-22 Refrigerant Supply and Timeline Pressure
While Francisco mentioned R-22 phase-out and high costs ($500/lb), he didn't create urgency around the dwindling supply: "The supply of R-22 gets tighter every year. If your compressor fails in 2 months and we have to special order the refrigerant, it could take 4-6 weeks to complete the repair." This framing would have created urgency for the replacement option.
