## Top Gaps & Coaching Priorities

### Gap 1: Missed Trust Reconnect (Decision band)
**Moment:** Customer says "You know, overcharging. That's why I asked you for a bit here before."  
**What should happen:** Tech should pause, ask a follow-up question ("What happened last time that felt off-track?" or "Tell me more about that—what specifically concerned you?"), listen, and then reposition: "I get it. Let me be clear: here's what I'm finding, here's why it's urgent, and here's exactly what we'd do and why."  
**Impact:** Rebuilds trust and frames the repair as necessary, not high-pressure.

### Gap 2: No Options Presented (Decision band)
**Moment:** After diagnosis, tech says "I'll put the report together" without offering a choice.  
**What should happen:** Offer a clear menu: "Based on what I found, we could do A [replace compressor only], B [replace both compressor and blower motor together], or C [try a repair and come back in 30 days to check the motor]. Which feels right to you?"  
**Impact:** Gives customer agency and often unlocks a same-day decision.

### Gap 3: Delayed Delivery (Deliver band)
**Moment:** "Give me about 20 minutes. I'll put the report together."  
**What should happen:** Return to customer within 10 minutes with written or verbal estimate, offer to review it together, ask what questions they have, and ask for the sale.  
**Impact:** Keeps momentum; reduces "we'll think about it" default.

## Expanded Coaching Advice

**For Next Call:**
1. **When you hear a price concern in the first 10 minutes, pause and reconnect.** Ask the customer about their last experience. Listen. Then frame your findings as a reset: "Here's what's different this time…"
2. **Always present two or three options before you step away.** Staged repairs, different brands, DIY risk vs. full replacement—give them a choice so they feel in control.
3. **Return with an estimate within 15 minutes.** Review it together. Get the objection. Answer it. Ask for the work. Same-day closes come from momentum, not follow-ups.
4. **Get a 5-star review when the job is done.** ("While I've got you, would you mind leaving us a quick review? It helps folks find us.")
