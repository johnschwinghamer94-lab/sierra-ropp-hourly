## FSG Bands

### Welcome: **Strong**
Robert opened warmly and set clear expectations. "Good morning. How are you?" He showed genuine interest (asking about their dog Ruby, mentioning he loves dogs). Established Sierra's philosophy early: "The goal is to not have you without ac... for me, coming out, is to give you an evaluation to be thorough and share everything that's going on with your system and then you can make a decision on how you would like to move forward." Set expectations about timeline: "Give me about 20 minutes. I'll put the report together."

### Assessment: **Strong**
Methodical, thorough questioning and check-ins. Asked key discovery questions: "Is this the first time something like this has happened?" and "Any repairs?" (which revealed the pigeon-incident history and the previous $1000 charge). During inspection, systematically checked filters, tested compressor windings with specialized equipment, examined the hard start device amperage, and inspected the blower motor. Each finding was explained to the customer as discovered—excellence in real-time check-ins.

### Decision: **Moderate**
Robert identified problems (compressor windings weakened, hard start over-amped, blower motor oil leak) and explained implications well. However, he did not build or present concrete options during the call, and missed reconnecting with the customer's key objection. When the customer said "You know, overcharging. That's why I asked you for a bit here before," Robert simply said "Gotcha" and moved on. This was the critical moment where the customer opened the door to address trust—Robert should have asked a clarifying question and tied his thorough evaluation as the solution.

### Deliver: **Solid**
Robert excelled at technical explanation: compressor windings, hard start device function, microprocessor relay, oil bearing failure, and history of components ($670 hard start, $411 microprocessor, $311 drain cleaning from Nov 2022). Honest about compressor cost range and realistic about lead times. Did not ask for a 5-star review.

## Outcome & Gaps

**Top Gap #1: Missed Reconnection on Trust**  
Customer revealed past negative experience and explicitly flagged concern about overcharging. Robert heard it but didn't rebuild confidence. A reconnecting question ("What specifically happened with that service? I want to make sure this time is different") would have shifted toward commitment.
