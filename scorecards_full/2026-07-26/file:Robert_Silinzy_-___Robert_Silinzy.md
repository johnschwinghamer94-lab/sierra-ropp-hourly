## Key Gap & Coaching

When customer asked "What did we pay $1600 for a couple years ago?"—that was a price sensitivity signal opening the door to decision momentum.

**What should have happened:**
Tech should have presented repair pathways right then: "I understand you've invested in this before. Here's what I'd recommend: [Option A] Compressor replacement... or [Option B] Also replace the blower motor proactively since it's showing failure signs. Costs would be $X–$Y. Let me formalize that in an estimate, but this is the direction I'd take."

**What actually happened:**
Tech checked invoices, then deferred to "I'll prepare an estimate in 20 minutes."

**Why it matters:**
Deferring killed momentum. Customer left without a mental picture of the repair or confidence the cost is justified—vulnerable to no-close when estimate arrives.

## Coaching

**For next time:** When a customer signals price concern, land the decision right then with 2–3 repair options, cost ranges, and trade-offs. Ask: "Does that feel like the right approach?" This builds confidence and locks direction before estimate arrives.

**This call's strength:** Solid technical mastery and real empathy with past-experience concern. Combine that with speed on presenting options at the decision moment.
