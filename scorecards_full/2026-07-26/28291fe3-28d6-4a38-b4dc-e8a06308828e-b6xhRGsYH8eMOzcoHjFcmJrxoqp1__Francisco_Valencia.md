## Gaps & Coaching Notes

1. **No review ask:** Consider closing with "We'd love your feedback if we nail this quote call too—a 5-star review on Google really helps us serve more families like yours."

2. **Gradual vs. sudden decline:** Could have asked "Did you notice it working okay a few weeks ago and then suddenly drop off, or has it been declining?" to rule out a single-failure event (e.g., compressor start failure) vs. the systemic mismatch. Doesn't change the diagnosis but sharpens the narrative.

3. **Dog urine detail handling:** Francisco mentioned it clearly, but customer didn't fully acknowledge. A quick follow-up check ("Just to make sure—you saw that yellow staining inside the condenser?") would confirm they understood the secondary risk.

Excellent diagnostic call. Francisco balanced technical authority with customer autonomy, presented options without bias, and turned a service call into a TGL. The weak spot is missing the 5-star ask—a small miss on an otherwise strong close.
