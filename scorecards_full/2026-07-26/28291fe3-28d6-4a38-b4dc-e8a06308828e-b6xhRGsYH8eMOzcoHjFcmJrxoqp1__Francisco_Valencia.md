## Coaching Notes

### What Francisco Did Right (WIN)
Francisco understood that education creates urgency. When he explained "they changed refrigerant laws in 2025, you can't just mix R22 with new refrigerant," the customer's mental model shifted from "maybe it'll last longer" to "oh, I actually need to replace it." 

Then he didn't stop there. He immediately moved to "let's schedule the install team." Even when customer said "I don't have the money," Francisco didn't take that as a hard no. He said essentially: "Okay, let's get the quote so when you're ready, you know exactly what it costs and what you're getting."

This is flipping a call: from "diagnostic" to "committed next step with install team."

**Key phrases:**
- "They changed the laws of refrigerant in 2025, so we can't even just change your indoor coils..."
- "So minimum, you have to replace that condenser and the coils inside..."
- "Are you guys able to send us quotes? ...if you want estimates for replacement, that guy has to come out."

### Next Call
Continue educating customers on the WHY, not just the WHAT. When you explain the business constraint or regulation (like R22 laws), customers move from "this is optional" to "oh, this is actually required." That creates urgency without pressure.
