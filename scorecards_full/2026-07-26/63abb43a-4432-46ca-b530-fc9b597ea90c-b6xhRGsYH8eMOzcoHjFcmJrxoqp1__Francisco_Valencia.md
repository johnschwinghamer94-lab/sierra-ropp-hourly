## Coaching Notes

### What Francisco Did Right (WIN)
Francisco understood momentum. When customer said "I want to check with warranty first," most techs would say "okay, call us back." Francisco instead anchored the next step: "Great—let's get the install team scheduled for X time so you have your appointment locked in, and you can call warranty between now and then to see what they'll cover."

This approach respects the customer's need to research while keeping them moving forward. It's a flip—you've moved from "diagnostic call with no next step" to "locked-in estimate appointment with install team."

**Key phrase:** "So for the purchase, if you want estimates for replacement, that guy has to come out." Instead of sending them away, Francisco proactively created the appointment path.

### Next Call
Continue this pattern. When you have a customer who needs to research or think, don't end the call without a locked-in next step. Always say "let me schedule that for you."
