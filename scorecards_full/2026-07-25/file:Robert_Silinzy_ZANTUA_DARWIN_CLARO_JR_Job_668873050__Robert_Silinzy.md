## Coaching Advice

**FIX — When you hear "So hot," slow down and ask the follow-up question that matters: "What temperature are you trying to reach, and how long does it normally take?" That simple clarification turns a complaint into a diagnostic pathway. Right now you're moving too fast through discovery, and the customer is left uncertain whether you even understand their problem. Take an extra 60 seconds in the welcome and assessment phases to build a shared definition of the issue—it costs nothing upfront but pays dividends in customer confidence and your ability to present a meaningful solution at the end of the call.**
