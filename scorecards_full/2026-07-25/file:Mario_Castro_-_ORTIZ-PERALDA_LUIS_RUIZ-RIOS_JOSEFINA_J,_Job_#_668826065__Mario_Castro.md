## Expanded Coaching Advice

**WIN opportunity missed:** The customer revealed a $12k system failing prematurely—a potentially high-value flip if Mario had reconnected to that pain and outlined next steps. Instead of "The compressor demands electricity when starting," Mario should have said: "I understand—that's a significant investment, and it's frustrating to see it acting up this soon. Let me finish my full inspection and I'll show you what's actually going on and your options to get this working reliably again."

This frames the inspection as purposeful (building to options) rather than purely diagnostic, and signals that Mario will return with a clear path—not just bad news.

**For next similar call:** When a customer mentions a premium, recent system already failing, treat that as an opening to build DECISION-step momentum:
1. Validate the frustration (quick empathy)
2. Confirm you'll investigate thoroughly
3. Preview that you'll present options (warranty path, repair cost-benefit, efficiency upgrade, service plan)
4. Return to them with specific findings + 2-3 clear options

This keeps the customer engaged and positions you for a flip or close rather than leaving them to wonder if they should just call someone else.
