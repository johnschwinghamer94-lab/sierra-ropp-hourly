## Coaching Notes (Expanded)
This was a drive-by inspection call that felt rushed. At 5 minutes, there was minimal time to establish expertise or build trust. The rep needed to:
1. **Reset expectations upfront** — "I'm here to do a thorough inspection of your cooling system and explain exactly what I find and what your options are"
2. **Ask discovery questions** — When/how often does the AC run? Any odd sounds? When was it last serviced?
3. **Show work-in-progress transparency** — "Let me grab my tools and take a few minutes to assess; I'll come back in and walk you through what I see"
4. **Close with clarity** — "Based on my inspection, here's what I recommend..." and schedule next step

Without these fundamentals, a short call often results in "unknown" outcomes. Next time, budget time proportional to the complexity and always leave with a scheduled commitment.
