## COACHING TIP

**WIN — Brandon used context to create options, not features to create choices:** Instead of "we have a basic plan or a premium plan," Brandon asked "what's your plan for the house?" That one question let him create two options that actually mattered to the customer. If they're selling soon, patch it. If they're staying, replace it. By tying the solution to the customer's actual situation (not just their budget), the customer felt understood rather than upsold. They said "yes" because the option fit their life, not because Brandon convinced them.

---

## COACHING ADVICE

Ask "What's your situation?" before you present options. Context first, solutions second. That's how you go from pitching to partnering.
