## Band Scores

### Welcome: Solid
Joe opens with a professional greeting ("Good afternoon, how are you today, ma'am?") and introduces himself clearly. He establishes expertise and sets expectations upfront: "Okay, Marsha, so I'm gonna conduct a thorough evaluation… if I do, would you like me to bring it to your attention or just file it away, please?" This shows competence and customer consideration. The rapport is natural and Marcy feels comfortable.

**Supporting quote:** "My name is Joe with Sierra Conditioning… I'm gonna conduct a thorough evaluation."

### Assessment: Solid
Joe performs a methodical inspection, explaining each step. He cleans the outdoor unit thoroughly ("took off all the panels all the way around the unit, got all the gunk and stuff… washed everything out for you"). He moves to the attic and identifies the core issue with clarity:

**Supporting quote:** "Your ductwork is splicing, and it's allowing a lot of the dust from the attic to end up inside the system. That's going to overwork components."

He educates the customer on what he found, explaining that the original ductwork is "definitely getting to that point of if the ductwork doesn't get addressed, we're going to be having a conversation about replacing this unit sooner than you would want to."

### Decision: Strong
Joe presents a tiered approach with clear options:
- Individual duct runs at $1,500 each (standard rate) or $1,236 with Costco member discount
- Priority 4 ducts in worst condition: $4,944 (with discount applied)
- Full system replacement: To be designed and quoted by Sean (sales manager)

When Marcy asks about material, Joe explains the upgrade: "It wouldn't be sheet metal… it would still be flex, but it wouldn't be this [old material]… Now it's a shiny, almost shimmery, almost metallic-like, and then the insulation inside is much thicker… R-8 because of where we live… R-8 is going to help better the airflow throughout the home."

When Marcy expresses concern about the AC being off during work, Joe overcomes that objection: "We're not going to tear everything out at once and then have the AC off for hours on end. We would start off with individual rooms… and let you guys know, like, hey, this one's going to be off."

Joe also handles the surge protector discussion calmly, explaining its value: "It's the reason why a lot of TVs come with surge protection… if something happens on the electrical side of everything with the home, instead of the device taking the damage, the thing charging it or the thing it's connected to takes the damage."

**Supporting quote on objection handling:** "Because it's going to be a lot cheaper to change out a light switch than it would be to change out a full furnace or a condenser."

### Deliver: Solid
Joe completes the maintenance work (filter replacement). He prepares paperwork and secures the appointment with Sean: "Next Friday? Okay… 1 to 3 would be perfect… Yes, it's the 31st."

Joe gets the customer's digital signature approving the appointment. He wraps professionally: "Let me button up the attic and I'll let you guys enjoy dinner, okay?"

**Gap:** No explicit ask for a 5-star review, and the closing could have been more assertive about the urgency of scheduling this estimate.
