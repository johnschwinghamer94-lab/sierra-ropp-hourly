## Expanded Coaching
Your technical knowledge is strong—you nailed the diagnosis and detailed explanation. Next time a customer raises an objection about a risk, reframe it as confidence in what we *will* do, not what we *won't*. Warranty language rebuilds trust faster than liability language erodes it.
