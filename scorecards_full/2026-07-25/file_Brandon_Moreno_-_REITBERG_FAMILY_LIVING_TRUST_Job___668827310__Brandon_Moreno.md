## Coaching Notes (Expanded)
This rep excelled at showing—not just telling—why the repair path is a financial trap. By displaying actual pressure gauges, explaining the $500-600 per pound Freon cost, and diagnosing compressor damage that won't reverse, the rep made replacement feel inevitable, not optional.

**Master moves:**
1. **Show measurements, not opinions** — Pressure gauges on camera + comparison to "green zone" is credible proof
2. **Quantify the hidden costs** — "$500-600 per pound" + "multiple pounds needed" = sticker shock for repair
3. **Explain damage that won't reverse** — Compressor damage is the close; once it's damaged, even the Freon refill doesn't fix it
4. **Offer a comparison path** — "Let me get pricing on both repair and replacement so you can weigh them" keeps customer engaged
5. **Set urgency gently** — "The other systems are showing early signs; you'll start seeing low pressure soon" primes customer for multi-system replacement

**Growth opportunity:** Could have moved faster to scheduling the comfort advisor instead of going through the attic inspection himself (though that diligence was valuable for credibility).

Overall, excellent flip driven by transparent diagnostics and financial clarity. This is how you convert a leak into a replacement.
