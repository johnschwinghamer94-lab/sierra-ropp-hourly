## Coaching Advice

**Strengths to reinforce:**
1. **Credibility-building honesty** — Your transparency about why Sierra doesn't do illegal side deals (reputation, licensing risk) is powerful trust-builder. Keep using it.
2. **Technical depth** — Explanations of compressor damage, warranty timelines, and cost structure give customers real information to weigh options.
3. **Empathy + options combo** — Acknowledging that customers don't have cash on hand, then offering financing, shows you understand the real constraint.

**Next-step priority:**
When a customer's objection is cost, and you offer financing as the solution, **always follow with a commitment question** before moving on. The pattern:
1. Acknowledge the objection
2. Present the financing option
3. **Ask: "Would seeing exact costs with financing help you make a decision today?"**
4. Get a yes/no or "let me check with my spouse" — not a vague "we'll think about it"

This transforms financing from a throwaway mention into a real path to close.
