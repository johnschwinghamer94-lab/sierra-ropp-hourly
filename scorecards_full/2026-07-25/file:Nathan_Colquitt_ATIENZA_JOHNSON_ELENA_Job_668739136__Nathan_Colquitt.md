## Coaching Advice

**FIX — In a 20-minute call, you've got just enough time to inspect and close. After you've identified the issues, don't drift into small talk or vague next steps. Instead, say: "We need to replace your compressor. It's $2,500 installed, we can do it Tuesday, and it comes with a 5-year parts warranty. Do you want to get on the schedule?" That directness respects the time you've invested and gives the customer a clear decision point. Right now, you're leaving customers in limbo after doing good diagnostic work.**
