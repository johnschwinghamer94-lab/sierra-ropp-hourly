## Coaching Notes (Expanded)
This rep did an expert job diagnosing the root cause (refrigerant type mismatch, compressor damage, multiple leaks) but failed at the decision/close stage. When repair options dried up, the rep had two choices:
1. Explain why replacement is the only viable path (compressor is failing, coils can't be replaced, EPA won't allow refill)
2. Offer financing to make replacement accessible

**What happened instead:** Rep explained the problem, confirmed unavailability with manager, but left customer in limbo—acknowledging the AC is doomed without offering a solution.

**Growth opportunities:**
1. **Pivot early** — "The R-22 coils are no longer available. That means replacement is actually your only path. Let me show you what that looks like and how we can make it affordable."
2. **Use the problem as urgency** — "Your compressor is already taking damage. Every day we wait, the risk grows. A replacement locks in 20 years."
3. **Offer financing immediately** — Don't leave it to the customer to ask. "We have financing options that start at $150/month for peace of mind."
4. **Schedule the replacement consultation** — "I'm going to get our install team to measure up and give you pricing. We can usually get you going this week."

This is a missed flip. The diagnosis was excellent, but the close required pivoting from repair to replacement + financing. Next time, when repair is off the table, own the replacement recommendation confidently. You have the customer's attention; use it.
