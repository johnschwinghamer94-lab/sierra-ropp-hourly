## Key Gaps

### Gap 1: Limited Option Presentation

## Coaching Advice

**FIX — After you diagnose the issue, pause and ask: "If we fix this, what's most important to you—getting it done this week, keeping costs down, or having a long-term warranty?" That question tells you whether the customer wants speed, value, or peace of mind, and it lets you pitch the right option. Right now you're leaving money on the table by not anchoring the customer's decision-making process early in the call.**
