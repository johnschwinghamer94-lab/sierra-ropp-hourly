## Coaching Notes (Expanded)
Cole's 87-minute call is a masterclass in using time to build an airtight case for replacement. He didn't just diagnose the problem; he explained why repair is a false economy:
- Leak search is expensive upfront
- Freon (R-410A) is costly to refill
- Compressor damage (122 amps) won't heal even after repair
- Blower motor already maxed out
- Freezing coil will cause ceiling damage soon

By stacking these problems, the rep made replacement feel like the only rational choice.

**Replicable strategies:**
1. **Show real measurements** — Pressure gauges, amp readings, king valve action (or inaction)
2. **Explain damage that won't reverse** — "Even after repair, the compressor damage stays. That number will rise."
3. **Connect to future failures** — "Blower motor at max; freezing coil causes ceiling leaks; second system showing early signs"
4. **Stack the case** — By listing 3-4 problems, replacement stops feeling like upsell and starts feeling like the only option
5. **Offer financing** — "You can get a new system for $150/month; repair today costs $4k and fails anyway"

**Growth opportunity:** Could have scheduled comfort advisor call during the visit (instead of waiting for customer to call back). But overall, the diagnostic and close were excellent.

This is an expert flip driven by transparent diagnostics and logical, not pushy, reasoning. Customer believes the replacement is necessary, not sold. That's the difference between a flip and a close—and this rep achieved it.
