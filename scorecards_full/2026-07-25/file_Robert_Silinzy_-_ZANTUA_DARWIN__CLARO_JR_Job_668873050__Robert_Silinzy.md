## Expanded Coaching

Temperature cycling on one zone while the other zone runs fine is a strong diagnostic clue pointing to either:
1. Thermostat/sensor issue on first floor
2. Ductwork/damper issue
3. System mismatch between zones

On next multi-zone call: Ask follow-ups like "Does it ever maintain the set temperature?" and "Have you tried adjusting the thermostat or turning on the fan manually?" These answers narrow options quickly and let you build a better value story tied to the customer's specific frustration.
