## Key Gaps

### Gap 1: Extended Diagnosis Without Closure
The 80-minute duration suggests thorough work but indicates difficulty transitioning from assessment to decision. Andrew likely explained problems in detail but didn't synthesize findings into a simple "here's what I recommend" statement.

### Gap 2: No Urgency or Timeline Created
Andrew didn't establish whether issues are urgent (fix this week) or can wait (monitor until next season). This ambiguity leaves customers paralyzed.

---

## Coaching Advice

**FIX — You've got great diagnostic skills—80 minutes shows thoroughness. But now learn to convert that into customer decisions. After the inspection, use this close: "I found two things: one is urgent (costs $X, can do it tomorrow), one is future (costs $Y, let's plan for next season). Which should we start with?" That structure moves customers from "I've learned a lot" to "I know what to do." Right now, you're leaving calls having done excellent work, but the customer's next call might be to a competitor who gives them permission to move forward.**
