## Coaching Notes

**What to repeat:**  
Cole's tire-analogy explanation ("putting air in a tire with a hole in it") was a masterclass in translating technical constraints into customer language. When the customer pushed back on the simple fix, this one line dissolved the objection because it made the impossibility visceral. Use this metaphor in training and in similar leak-based calls.

**What to improve:**  
1. Always close the Deliver phase with a 5-star ask, even on diagnostic calls where no money changes hands.  
2. On flipped calls with urgency (party tomorrow), amplify the urgency for the replacement path: "This actually means replacement is your best move because we can have you set before you need it."  
3. Consider a softer opening: "I know this is frustrating—thank you for letting me take a look" (empathy before expertise) to warm up the relationship earlier.

**Next call:**  
Watch for similar objection patterns (customer wanting the cheap/fast option despite diagnosis). Practice the metaphor + legal/practical barriers + positive pivot on 2-3 calls with observers.
