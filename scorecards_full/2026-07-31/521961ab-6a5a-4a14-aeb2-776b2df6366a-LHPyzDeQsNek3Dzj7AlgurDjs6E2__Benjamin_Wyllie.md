## Coaching Tip

**FIX — Tech spent most of call on personal chatter about sports and casinos rather than diagnosing AC problem; could've asked customer about thermostat issue symptoms, how long it's been failing, or what they've tried so far.**

You have genuine warmth with customers—that's a strength. But lead with the problem. Start with: "So your AC isn't coming on in the morning—how long has that been happening? Let me check the system and I'll tell you exactly what's going on." Build rapport while diagnosing, not instead of it.
