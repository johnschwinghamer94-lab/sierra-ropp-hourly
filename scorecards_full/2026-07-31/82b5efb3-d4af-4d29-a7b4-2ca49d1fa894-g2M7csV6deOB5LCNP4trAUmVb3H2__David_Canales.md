## Coaching Note

**Moment:** Customer asked, "Knowing that I'm gonna probably replace the unit, you know, in the winter, like, do I even do anything with that primary?"

**What happened:** David said "I'd have to look up the pricing" and deferred the conversation.

**What could have turned it:** Reconnect to the concrete risk you'd already described ("You'll have a giant hole in your ceiling") and either:
1. Quantify the timeline risk: "If the secondary clogs before winter, that emergency pan is your only safety net—let me unclog the primary as a precaution," OR
2. Propose a lower-cost interim step: "We could clear both drains today for $X, which buys you peace of mind until the replacement."

The customer was actually asking for permission to do nothing—which is fine if the risk doesn't justify it. But you'd already shown them the risk. Reconnecting that concern back to the $500 decision would have either earned the approval or given them real confidence in their "wait and see" choice.
