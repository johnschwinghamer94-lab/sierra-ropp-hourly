**Skipped the expectations roadmap before inspection** — You asked good discovery questions, but then moved straight to inspection without the essential step: setting a clear roadmap. "I'll check the indoor and outdoor units, then we'll review what I find and talk through your options" takes 10 seconds and signals to the customer that there's a next step. Without it, they may not expect you to come back with options or may feel like you're just doing the inspection and disappearing.

## Recommendation
