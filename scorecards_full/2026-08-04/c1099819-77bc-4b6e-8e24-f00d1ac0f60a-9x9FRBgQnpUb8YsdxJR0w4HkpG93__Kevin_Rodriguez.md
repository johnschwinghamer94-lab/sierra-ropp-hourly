## Coaching

**Exceptional Win:**
This is a masterclass in objection handling. When Larry mentioned his daughter's AC failed and wasn't covered, most techs would have just said "sorry, next time get the warranty." You did something smarter:

*What you did right:*  
1. **Heard the pain** — Daughter's $5k+ AC failure, not covered. That's expensive regret.
2. **Validated his current carefulness** — "You change filters every 2 months. I get it."
3. **Offered the fix to his fear** — SAM membership isn't just maintenance; it's the extended warranty protection he wishes he'd had.
4. **Closed on the emotion** — "15% off repairs alone pays for itself" isn't a feature—it's insurance against his daughter's mistake.

*The exact moment:*  
Customer: "I didn't get the extended warranty."  
Your response: You didn't dismiss it. You validated his diligence ("you change filters") and offered SAM as the solution to the exact problem he experienced with his daughter's unit.

**Why it worked:**  
You recognized that his objection wasn't "I don't want to spend money." His objection was "I don't want to get stuck like my daughter did." SAM membership solved his real fear, not just the tune-up.

---

**Recommendation:**  
This is your close framework. When a customer mentions extended warranty concerns or past failures, always reframe the upsell as the insurance they should have bought last time. It converts objections into wins.
