## Coaching Advice

**Wins:** Francisco and Jose did excellent technical work. The diagnosis was thorough, the explanation of options was clear, and the financing discussion was complete. The system brands conversation was also good—explaining why Lennox works better in Vegas than Carrier (parts availability from West Coast mfg) shows expertise.

The customer absolutely understood that replacement was better than repairs. That's a victory. The problem wasn't the sales process—it was leaving without a commitment.

**Fix:** When a customer says "I want to research and compare," that's your moment to create mild urgency and a reason to commit now. In an emergency repair situation (no AC in summer), the customer has a timeline pressure. Francisco didn't lean into "you need cold air TONIGHT, not after you've researched for 3 days." A small commitment device (quote good for 24 hours, "we can install tomorrow if you say yes now") might have moved the needle.

**For next time:** Watch for the "I'll research" comment. That usually means "I'm going to shop around and maybe not come back." Counter with: 1) validate it's smart, 2) show why your option is already the best value, 3) create a small time constraint (quote expiration, schedule availability), 4) make the "yes" decision easier than the "shop around" path.
