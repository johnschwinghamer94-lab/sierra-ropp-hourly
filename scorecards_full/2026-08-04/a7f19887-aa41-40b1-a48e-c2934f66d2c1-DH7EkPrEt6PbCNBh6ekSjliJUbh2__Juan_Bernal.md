## Coaching

**Missed Moment:**
When you discovered the circuit breaker was tripped, that's actually a red flag that should trigger a diagnostic conversation. Circuit breaker trips usually mean either:
1. An overload (system pulling too much power)
2. A fault in the system (compressor issue, capacitor, etc.)

You should have asked: "This breaker being tripped is interesting. Has this happened before? Let me run some quick electrical tests to make sure your system isn't working too hard."

Then, if you find an issue: "I'm seeing signs of a component working harder than it should. This could become a safety issue. Would you like me to write up a repair estimate so you can plan for that?"

**The Gap:**
Discovery + diagnosis ≠ solutions. The circuit breaker trip was your opening to either close a repair or flip to a TGL for an estimate visit.
