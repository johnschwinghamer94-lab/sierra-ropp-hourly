## Coaching

**Strength:**
You didn't dismiss Deborah's concern about AC not getting below 73°F. Too many techs would say "that's normal" and move on. You listened and confirmed it's a real limitation in extreme heat.

**The Missed Flip:**
When Deborah said "I just keep it at 73" because it won't go lower, that's an opening for a soft upsell:

*What you could have said:*  
"At 110 degrees, most systems hit that ceiling. But there are some things we could check—refrigerant level, airflow efficiency, even adding a second unit to the bedroom if you use it a lot. Would you be interested in a quick efficiency check, or should I have one of our specialists give you an upgrade estimate?"

**The Gap:**
You identified a comfort problem but didn't offer solutions. Deborah already decided to accept 73°F as her "new normal," which means she's resigned to discomfort. That's a signal she'd consider a fix if one were offered.

**Recommendation:**  
Cooling limitations during heat waves are common problems customers accept until someone offers an alternative. Your close should be: "This limitation is normal in extreme heat, but if you'd like to explore options—better efficiency, zoning, or a second unit—I can set you up with an estimate. Worth thinking about?"
