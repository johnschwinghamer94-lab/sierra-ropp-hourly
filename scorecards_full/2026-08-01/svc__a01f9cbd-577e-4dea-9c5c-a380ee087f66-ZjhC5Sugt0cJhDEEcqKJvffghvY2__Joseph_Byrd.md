## Expanded Coaching

**The Missed Moment:** After the reset resolved the immediate issue and customer said "we're cooling now," tech had 5-10 minutes left in the call to ask for the full evaluation. Instead of saying "I'm gonna go check pressures and everything out there," tech should have said: "Your system just got stuck on a time delay, probably from a surge—that's actually pretty common in summer. Before we say it's fixed, I want to check pressures and your electrical to make sure nothing else got damaged. Should I do that now?" This is a direct ask that confirms scope and value, not just assumptions.

**Why It Matters:** Customer spent $30K on this system and was relieved it's working again. At that moment, they would likely have approved a $200-300 diagnostic if framed as "verification that everything's safe." Tech had all the power but didn't convert the goodwill into next step confirmation.
