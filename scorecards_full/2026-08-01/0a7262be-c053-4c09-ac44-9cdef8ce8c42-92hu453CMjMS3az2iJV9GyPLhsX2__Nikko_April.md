## Top Gaps & Coaching Advice

**Gap 1: Scope Escalation Without Reset**
Jumped from fan motor diagnosis ($1,100) to full system replacement ($20,000) without pausing to reset customer expectations or explain the logic step-by-step.

**Coaching:**
Break the diagnostic into stages: "Fan motor is $1,100 to fix. Now let me test the compressor response to see if there are secondary issues." Let *each result* inform the next question, not back up the full diagnosis at once.

**Gap 2: Lost Credibility Mid-Call**
Customer felt pitched rather than diagnosed. The emotional tone shifted from "here's what I found" to "here's what you need to buy."

**Coaching:**
Stay in diagnostician mode throughout. "Here's what I'm seeing on the compressor side... this tells me the compressor is under stress. That's separate from the motor repair. Let me explain what that means for your options." Frame it as evidence, not as upselling.
