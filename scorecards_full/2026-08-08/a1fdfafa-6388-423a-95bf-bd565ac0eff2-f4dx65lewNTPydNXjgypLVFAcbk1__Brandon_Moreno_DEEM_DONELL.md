## Top Gaps & Coaching

1. **"Explanation as closure" trap** — Brandon spent most of the call explaining why the problem is hard and why other companies failed. This is excellent rapport-building but zero sales work. Customer leaves feeling understood but not helped.

2. **Missed authority moment** — This customer is primed to flip: they've tried 5 other companies and are frustrated. They WANT a hero. Brandon had the rapport and the moment but didn't say: "Here's what we're different. And here's how we solve it."

3. **No option presentation** — After 27 minutes, Brandon should have said: "Okay, so here's what I recommend: [Option A/B]. We'll need [X] and it will take [Y days]. Cost is [Z]. Let's get you scheduled."

## Top 1-2 Gaps with Quotes

1. **Solution avoidance** — Customer: "I've had five air conditioning companies out here. Everybody tells you something different." Brandon responded with sympathy but no remedy pathway. Should have said: "I hear that. Let me tell you what our five-company-fix plan looks like."

2. **No ownership of fix** — Brandon explained the problem's complexity but never said "We can fix this" with specificity. High-frustration customers need clarity and confidence from the tech, not just validation of their frustration.

## Coaching Tip

**FIX — Built good rapport and diagnosed a complex systemic issue clearly, but focused entirely on explaining why five other companies couldn't solve it rather than proposing Sierra's solution.**

Brandon's rapport with this frustrated customer was gold. But he used that trust to build *sympathy*, not *momentum*. After Brandon spent time on "everybody tells you something different," he should have pivoted: 

"Here's what I found: [Root cause]. Here's why other guys missed it: [Explanation]. Here's how we fix it: [Solution A or B]. This is what it costs and how long it takes. Let's schedule you for [date]."

Instead, Brandon left the customer validated but still searching for answers. In a multi-company-failure situation, the tech who says "I've got the answer and here's how we do it" wins the close.
