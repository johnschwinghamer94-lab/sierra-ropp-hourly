## Coaching Tip

**FIX — Diagnosed compressor and electrical issues thoroughly and explained technical details well, but never created a second option or reconnected to customer's concern about warranty coverage before ending.**

Andrew's technical depth is a strength, but he got absorbed in diagnosis and forgot to bridge back to the customer's emotional state. After explaining the failed capacitor, he should have said: "Here's what this means: we can replace just the capacitor—that won't affect your warranty—or we can do a full system health check and give you an upgrade path. Which sounds better for your situation?"

That simple pivot converts a 59-minute diagnosis call into a flip.
