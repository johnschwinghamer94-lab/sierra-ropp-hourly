## Key Gaps & Coaching

**Gap 1: Passive acceptance of customer's scope**
When the customer said "I don't really need to look at anything else other than just replace this," Robert agreed immediately. That's the moment to slow down and gather diagnostic detail:
- "I hear you want replacement as an option—before I finalize that, help me understand: What made you decide it needs replacement? Any symptoms recently?"
- This builds trust and sets up a stronger recommendation later.

**Gap 2: No diagnostic narrative**
Robert jumps to inspection without asking the customer to describe the problem. Symptoms guide diagnosis and create urgency for options later.

**Coaching for next call:**
When a customer rushes you to "just replace" or "just fix," that's your entry point for discovery, not your exit. Ask "What symptoms made you call today?" and listen—then you can offer a diagnostic inspection that earns their trust and sets up options credibly. On this job, leading with discovery could have revealed a $200 fix instead of a $2K replacement.
