**What Nathan said:** "I'm not gonna do any work without your approval today... the goal is to deliver a five star experience. If I'm not meeting that, you shop."
**Why it worked:**
- Set clear boundary on work authorization (reassurance)
- Declared goal upfront (five-star experience)

- Made it about them, not about the sale
- Showed confidence in service quality

**Impact:** Customer's anxiety visibly decreased. They went from worried to cooperative. This is the foundation for when you later present options or repair recommendations—the customer already trusts you won't surprise them.

## Coaching Advice (Expanded)

**WIN — Customer was worried and uncertain about thermostat issue. You set clear boundaries immediately: 'I'm not gonna do any work without your approval' and 'goal is deliver five-star experience.' That calm, transparent expectation-setting reassured them right at the start. Trust foundation built early.**

This is a textbook welcome/expectations play. You:
1. Set the control boundary (no work without approval)
2. Declared the goal upfront (five-star)
3. Gave power back to customer ("If I'm not meeting it, you shop")

Result: Customer went from anxious to cooperative. When you come back with findings and options, this trust foundation means they're listening to solve, not defending their choices.

Use this exact approach on your next three-unit or multi-zone system where customer is confused. It works because it's honest, clear, and customer-centered.
