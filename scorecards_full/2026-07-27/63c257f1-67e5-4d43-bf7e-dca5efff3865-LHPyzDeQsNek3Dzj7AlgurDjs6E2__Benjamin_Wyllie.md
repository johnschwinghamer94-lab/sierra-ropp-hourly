## Key Wins & Coaching

### Win 1: Powerful Diagnosis Metaphor + Immediate Solution
Benjamin didn't say "Your compressor is bad." He used a relatable analogy: "like a car engine or an artery got clogged going to the heart, causing the heart to fail."

This made the failure mechanism undeniable and understandable. Customer immediately got it and said "We're not fixing. That thing's so old."

Instead of defending repair, Benjamin immediately offered: "I can get a guy out here for that... today." This pivot from defense to forward motion kept momentum and prevented the conversation from stalling on the bad news.

### Win 2: Preemptive Objection Handling
Customer never had to say "We can't afford $5,953" or "We're selling soon." Benjamin addressed the underlying concern proactively:
- "due to the age and condition of the system, overpayin by about 15 on your utility bills" — made cost of inaction clear
- "I can get them out here today" — removed timeline as an objection
- Full report walk-through with visual evidence — removed uncertainty objection

By the time customer might have raised an objection, Benjamin had already positioned both options (repair now if needed to limp by, or replacement for permanent fix) as reasonable paths forward.

### Win 3: Lightness Amid Difficult News
Benjamin acknowledged the emotional weight: "I'm just kind of adding salt to the wound at this point... But that's not like, what the intentions were." This humor+empathy combo kept the conversation feeling collaborative, not adversarial.

Customer stayed engaged and receptive instead of defensive.
