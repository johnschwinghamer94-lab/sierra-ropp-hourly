## Coaching Advice (Expanded)

**WIN — When customer shared his wife passed in April, you paused with respect ('excuse me') instead of pushing through. That human moment shown at vulnerable time builds trust when the customer needs work done. Small gesture, big impact on rapport for major purchase decision.**

This is the leadership moment in every sales call. When a customer shares personal loss or hardship:
1. Pause (acknowledge you heard)
2. Show respect (don't minimize)
3. Return to purpose (continue helping with what they need)

You did all three naturally. That's why customers say "yeah" to big decisions—because they trust the person, not just the company.

On your next call when someone shares vulnerable info (loss, financial stress, health issue), use Robert's approach: pause, acknowledge, move forward with dignity. It costs nothing and it's everything in a major purchase decision.
