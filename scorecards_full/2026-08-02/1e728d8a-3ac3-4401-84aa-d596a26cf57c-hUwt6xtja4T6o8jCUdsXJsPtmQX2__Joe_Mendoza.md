## Gap Analysis

### Primary Gap: Objection Validation
**The moment:** Customer said, "When I would flip my breakers over here, they would affect [the noise]."  
**Joe said:** "I don't think that's the case."  
**What happened:** Customer pushed back hard: "I'm telling you that's what happened, okay? I can't explain it. I'm a repairman also."  

**The miss:** Leading with correction feels dismissive, even when technically correct. Better approach:

**What Joe could have said:** "I hear you — you've observed that pattern. Let me explain why these circuits shouldn't interact, but I'm going to check everything to make sure."

This validates the customer's experience while explaining the technical reality. By the end, the actual power issue vindicated Joe's reasoning, but the FSG move of validating-before-correcting was skipped.

## Coaching Tip
**FIX —** When addressing customer concerns about your diagnosis, validate their observation first before explaining why it might not tell the whole story; this keeps them on your side while you deliver the real finding.
