## EXPANDED COACHING ADVICE

### Master the Pre-Qualified Payment Plan Close

When a customer expresses cost concerns, your pre-qualified status from the office is a powerful closer. Use it like Fabian did:

**Pattern:** "Acknowledge the concern → Remind them of pre-qualification → Move forward"

**Instead of:** Waiting for the customer to ask, or saying "We offer payment plans"

**Try:** "Yeah, she already confirmed you're pre-qualified for our payment plan. So the $[cost] for the compressor is just about $[monthly amount] a month. Let's get you taken care of."

### Why This Works
- **No discovery phase:** Customer already approved; you're not asking
- **Normalizes financing:** "She already got you qualified" means it's standard, not special
- **Redirects focus:** From "Can I afford it?" to "What are we fixing?"
- **Builds trust:** Office coordination signals professional operation

### Script for Different Cost Scenarios

**If customer hesitates at price:** "Look, I know that's not small change. The good news is you're pre-qualified for payments, so it spreads over the year. You're looking at about [X] a month, and the alternative is no AC in this heat. Let's move forward."

**If customer wants time to think:** "I understand. But here's the thing—you're already pre-qualified. If we get this done tonight, you're cooling again in a couple hours. If you wait, we're booked for [days], and you're running the AC down more. Can we do this now?"

**If customer asks about the TXV upgrade:** "We'll replace the compressor first, run the pressures, and see if the TXV is the culprit. If it is, we can add it. But let's start with the compressor since we know that failed."

### Sequence for Cost-Conscious Customers

1. **Diagnose completely:** Identify all issues (compressor + potential TXV)
2. **Address the primary issue first:** "We need to replace the compressor. That's the immediate fix."
3. **Offer optional upgrade:** "The TXV might be next—we'll see when we test."
4. **Present the cost:** "The compressor is $[X]. You're pre-qualified for a payment plan."
5. **Remove friction:** "Let's get started. You'll be cool again tonight."

### Why Fabian's Close Worked

Fabian did three things perfectly:
1. **Assumed approval:** Told customer they were already qualified (not asked)
2. **Kept momentum:** Moved straight to "let's get started" without waiting for hesitation
3. **Offered choice on upgrade:** TXV was optional, compressor was the priority

This is the gold standard for cost-sensitive closes.
