## Gaps & Coaching Notes

### Gap #1: Limited Diagnosis Explanation
The compressor failure seemed predetermined by dispatch. Fabian did not explain what pressures were out of range, what failure mode he observed, or why the TXV was the root cause. Customers who understand the "why" are more likely to approve upgrades like the TXV.

### Gap #2: No Membership Offer
The customer experienced a major compressor failure—a sign their system may need proactive maintenance. A Service Agreement (SAM) with regular inspections could prevent future failures. This was not mentioned.

### Gap #3: Soft Close
While the compressor repair is moving forward, Fabian did not ask directly: "Does that work for you?" or "Shall I start the repair?" He instead said "And we'll go from there," which is an implicit close but not the explicit ask the rubric expects.
