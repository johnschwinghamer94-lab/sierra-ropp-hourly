## Coaching Advice

**FIX — Customer expressed compressor concern directly; tech confirmed diagnosis but didn't pause to quote repair/replacement costs or present options before leaving for tools.**

When a customer brings up a high-ticket item like compressor, that's your selling moment—not your exit cue. Before you go grab tools, say: "Here's what I'm seeing on repair versus replacement, and here's what each option costs." Show three clear choices with pricing. Then ask which direction they want to go. Right now you're leaving money on the table by moving straight from diagnosis to work. Take the middle step—options and pricing—and watch your close rate jump.
