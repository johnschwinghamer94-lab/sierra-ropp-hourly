## Expanded Coaching

Adrian's strengths: accurate technical diagnosis and calm demeanor.

**What needs to improve:**

1. **Lead with expectations, not mystery.** At the start: "Here's what I'm going to check, here's what it might cost, and here's how long it takes." This builds trust and shortens the sales cycle.

2. **Explain findings in customer language.** When you find a compressor not running, say "The compressor should make a humming sound when the AC kicks in—yours isn't, so we're testing the capacitor, which is like a starter for the compressor. If that's it, we can replace it today for around $150–200."

3. **Always propose options, even during diagnosis.** "While I'm testing this, are you interested in an upgraded capacitor that lasts longer, or would you prefer we stick with the standard replacement?"

4. **Close before you walk away.** Never end a discovery phase without an ask. "Does that sound like a plan?" or "Should I go ahead and test the capacitor?" keeps the conversation in your court.
