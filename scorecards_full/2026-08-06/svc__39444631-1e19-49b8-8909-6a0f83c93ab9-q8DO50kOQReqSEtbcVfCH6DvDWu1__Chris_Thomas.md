## Evidence & Gaps

**Strongest moment:** Diagnosis and validation. Chris showed the burnt switch, explained the weld/constant-running consequence, and the customer immediately said "Yeah, we've been having that problem already." Real pain point, clear solution.

**Top gaps:**
1. **Deferred when should've closed:** Customer confirmed the problem is actively happening ("My dad will be here... the AC hasn't shut off"). Chris should have said: "Since your system should be under warranty, here's what I recommend: I can reach out to the manufacturer to expedite the registration, and then we can get this fixed for you today or this week — without the labor cost if the warranty's good." Instead, he kicked it back to the customer.
2. **No warranty-close strategy:** Chris checked warranty, found it unregistered, and said "call them back." But he could have offered to call on their behalf or set a callback to confirm warranty before customer hangs up.
3. **No membership mention:** A 40-minute maintenance call with an identified repair need is a perfect time to mention the membership's 15% repair discount.

## Coaching Insight
FIX — When you find a problem the customer confirms they're experiencing ("We've been having that problem already"), and you can fix it, close it: "Your system should be under warranty at 2–3 years. Let me confirm that registration today, and if it's covered, we'll get this fixed for you at no labor cost." Don't make them call the manufacturer and call you back.
