## Coaching Gaps (Top 2)

### Gap 1: Missed the Close Signal
Customer said "I'll take them off for sure"—that is the CLOSE SIGNAL. You should have stopped right there and said:
- "Awesome, let me just get your signature on this membership agreement real quick, and you're all set. Starting today, your cooling service is included."
- Then get the pen, get the signature, get the payment method
- THEN go to the roof

Instead, you said "we'll talk about it" and left. Now you've got to come back, re-engage, overcome the "let me think about it" that always follows when tech leaves the room.

### Gap 2: No Soft Close Before Roof Inspection
Before leaving for the roof, you should have said: "While I'm up there, just so you know what to expect—if I find anything that needs attention, like a low refrigerant charge or a capacitor wearing out, I'll come back with options and pricing. We can handle it today or schedule it whenever works for you." This sets the frame for the sale BEFORE you deliver bad news.
