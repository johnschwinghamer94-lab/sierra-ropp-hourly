## Expanded Coaching Advice
You had strong option presentation with great details on membership benefits. The gap is converting it to a yes. Instead of "think about it," a direct ask like "Does $16/month work for your budget for all those benefits?" or a trial close like "I'll add you to membership right now" puts the customer in decision mode. Even if they say no, you can ask what specifically concerns them, which often unlocks the real objection.
