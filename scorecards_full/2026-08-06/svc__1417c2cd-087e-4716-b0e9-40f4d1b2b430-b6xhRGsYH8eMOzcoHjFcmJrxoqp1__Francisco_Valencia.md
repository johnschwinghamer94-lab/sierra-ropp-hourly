## Coaching Tip

They walked you through the airflow issue step-by-step (bathroom vent check, bedroom vent check, comparing to baseline performance), and you didn't interrupt. Your commitment to "go grab my things, hop up there, and see what I can do" shows you're taking ownership. The customer's gratitude ("Thank you though", "Much appreciated") indicates they felt heard and believed you'd help.

FIX — Set expectations before you go upstairs. Say: "Let me grab my tools and get up there. Right now you're walking off without clarity, which means the customer is wondering if you'll do $500 of work or $50 of work.
