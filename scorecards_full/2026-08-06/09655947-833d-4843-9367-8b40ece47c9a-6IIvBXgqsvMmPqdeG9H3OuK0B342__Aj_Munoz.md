## Coaching Guidance

**Strength:** AJ is personable and excellent at explaining complex membership benefits clearly. Customer understood the value and asked follow-up questions, showing real interest.

**Development Area:** Convert interest into action. When a customer engages by asking clarifying questions, that's the signal to ask for the sale directly. Replace "think about it while" language with a closed question: "Can I get you enrolled today?" or "Does this sound like something you'd like to start with?"

**Next Call:** After explaining any service option, watch for the customer's engagement signals (questions, affirmations like "okay"). When you see them, move immediately to the ask. Don't hand off a brochure and walk away—ask for a yes-or-no decision while they're engaged.
