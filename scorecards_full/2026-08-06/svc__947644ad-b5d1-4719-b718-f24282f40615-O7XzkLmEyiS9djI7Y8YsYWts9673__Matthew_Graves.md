## Band Details

### Welcome: Moderate
**Greeting Without Upfront Expectations**

Matt opened professionally:
> "Hi, how's it going? I'm Matt with Sierra, here for some AC trouble."

However, he did not set clear upfront expectations about the visit's scope, cost to diagnose, or estimated timeframe. The customer immediately volunteered the issue (cracking noise), and Matt proceeded into diagnosis mode without anchoring expectations—a best practice missed.

---

### Diagnosis: Strong
**Extensive Investigation and Clear Technical Explanation**

Matt invested significant time listening and testing:
- Asked clarifying questions about when the noise occurs
- Investigated prior installation history (unit upgraded from 2.5 to 3.0 tons)
- Listened directly to the noise source inside the wall
- Moved to inspect the outdoor unit location and electrical box

**Root Cause Identified and Explained:**
> "Sometimes those get shifted and sometimes they're right next to like a stud in the wall or something like that. And so when it kicks on and there's that initial vibration, or sometimes when it's running... it'll smack against that."

Matt then proposed a solution—a soft start device that would reduce startup vibration:
> "because it's starting out gently...it shouldn't have as much...oomph going up that line."

He tested the device in place and confirmed results before closing:
> "Yeah, just get that starting up nice and gentle... You didn't hear it? No. Really? Amazing."

The diagnosis moved from problem identification to solution validation—textbook thorough work.

---

### Options: Solid
**Two Primary Paths Presented, Below the 4+ Target**

Matt presented distinct offerings but fell short of the 4+ option target:

1. **Soft Start Only**: $1,429 (straight installation)
2. **Soft Start + Membership**: $1,231 (with $300 membership discount, effectively bundled)
3. **Membership Alone** (implied for ongoing AC/heating/plumbing maintenance): $16/month ($192/year)

The options weren't framed as a classic "good/better/best" hierarchy. Instead, Matt positioned membership as adding value ($300 discount) to the soft start purchase. The customer was asked: "are you more inclined towards just doing the straight install, or would you be interested in, uh, the, the maintenance program that has that discount?" 

This is a binary choice, not a rich menu of 4+ options.

---

## Coaching Tip

**WIN — ** When customer hesitated on cost ("how much...with...discount?"), Matt reframed the price as a package value: "instead of $1,429...with discount...brings it to $1,231" AND highlighted membership bundling (4 AC/heating/plumbing visits yearly, $192/year total), converting price anxiety into perceived package value—earning both the soft start approval ($1,231) and annual membership commitment.

## Gaps & Opportunities

1. **Weak Welcome:** No upfront expectations set about scope or cost. "I'm here for AC trouble" leaves the customer uncertain about diagnostic fees, solution range, or timeline. Stronger opener: "I'll inspect what's causing the noise, take about 30 minutes, and run you through what we find and any options to fix it."

2. **Limited Options:** Only 2-3 distinct paths presented. Could have offered: (a) soft start only, (b) soft start + 1-year membership, (c) soft start + 3-year membership (if available), (d) just ongoing membership monitoring (no install). More choices = higher perceived control and close rate.

3. **Membership Auto-Pay Surprise:** Customer was informed mid-call that membership includes auto-pay. While accepted, this should be front-loaded in the membership explanation to avoid last-minute friction.

---

## Summary

Matt excelled at diagnosis and close—he listened extensively, identified the root cause, tested the solution, and sealed the deal with membership value framing. The weak link is options quantity and weak upfront expectations. The win came from reframing cost as a bundled package (soft start + membership value), which neutralized price objection and locked in both hardware and recurring revenue.
