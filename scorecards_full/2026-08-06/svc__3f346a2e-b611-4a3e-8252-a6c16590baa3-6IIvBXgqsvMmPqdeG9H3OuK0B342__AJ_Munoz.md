## Coaching Advice

**FIX — Customer revealed previous $500 bill from another tech's repair; opportunity to offer preventive maintenance plan was missed, then tech ended call before presenting solutions.**

When a customer mentions a costly prior repair, that's your entry point to talk about prevention. Frame it: "That's exactly what our maintenance members avoid—we catch small issues before they turn into big bills. Let me show you how that plan would work." Then present 3-4 options (repair only, repair + filters, upgrade to full maintenance agreement) with specific pricing. End with a direct ask: "Which option works best for you?" This 10-minute call could have been a $40/month membership + a service order if you'd stayed and built value.
