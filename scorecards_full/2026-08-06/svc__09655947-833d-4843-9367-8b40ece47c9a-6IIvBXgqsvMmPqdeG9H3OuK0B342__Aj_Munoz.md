## Key Gaps & Coaching

**Gap 1: Soft close instead of direct ask**
AJ presented the membership beautifully with clear value, but the response "think about it" is not a closing technique. The customer needed an actual decision point: "Would you like to start the membership today?"

**Gap 2: Limited options for a 6-minute call**
While the membership is well-explained, no mention of filter upgrades, duct cleaning, or other add-on services that could have rounded out the offer.

---

## Coaching Advice

**FIX — After explaining all the membership benefits, transition to a direct question: "This membership makes a lot of sense for you given your home age and our prior service history. Can we get you signed up today?" The customer's engagement level was high; a direct ask instead of 'think about it' would likely have converted the sale.**

Customer had a good prior relationship with Sierra (had AC work done before) and was receptive to the pitch. The soft close left the decision hanging.
