## Expanded Coaching

**Strengths:** Fabian is an excellent diagnostician. His explanation of the mineral-water problem is clear, accurate, and builds customer understanding. He is professional, completes the work, and verifies the fix. His technical knowledge is solid.

**What to work on:**

1. **Diagnosis without prescription is incomplete.** When you find the root cause, the customer expects (and wants) a recommendation to fix it. Fabian said, "Hard water causes mineral buildup that clogs filters," but never said, "So here's what I recommend..." This leaves the customer thinking, "Okay, now what do I do?"

2. **Cross-sell in the moment.** After identifying a hard-water issue, you have plumbing's phone number. Offer it: "Our plumbing team can assess whether a water softener would help. Want me to have them call you?"

3. **Always close with a preventive offer.** Even on maintenance-only calls, close with: "Everything looks great. To make sure this ice doesn't come back, would you like us to do a 3-month follow-up check?" or "We have an emergency membership—if this happens again at midnight, we'll come out at no charge. Want to add that?"

**Action steps:**

- Practice this pattern: **"Here's the problem → Here's why it happened → Here's how to prevent it."**
- If you diagnose a root cause involving another trade (plumbing, electrical, insulation), offer a cross-sell in that moment.
- Always propose a follow-up plan or membership before you leave a customer with a known recurring issue.
