## Key Gaps & Coaching

**Gap 1: Ambiguous close**
The customer's statement "I'll take them off for sure" is unclear—does this mean filters, membership, or both? Chris should have clarified: "So I'll put you down for the 3-inch filters and the membership starting today?" to confirm commitment.

**Gap 2: Limited options range**
While the 3-inch filters and membership are good offers, there's no mention of financing, additional services, or upgrade paths. The GAID call showed how expanding the menu to 5+ options increases perceived value and close rates.

**Gap 3: Premature membership pitch**
Chris mentions membership "when I'm after. I'm done with that, we'll. We'll talk about it." This pushes the conversation back and weakens the close momentum at the end of the call.
