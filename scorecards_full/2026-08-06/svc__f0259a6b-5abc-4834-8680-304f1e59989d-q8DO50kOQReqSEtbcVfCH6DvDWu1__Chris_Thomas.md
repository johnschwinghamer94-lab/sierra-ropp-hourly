**Key win:** Visual diagnosis + consequence explanation
Chris showed the burnt voltage switch side-by-side with the good one, played the electrical hum audio, and explained the $6-8k compressor replacement risk. This built urgency and confidence in the solution.

**Key win:** Multiple price points and value ladder
Rather than one option, Chris offered mechanical replacement, upgraded computer solution, soft start kits, and filter options. This let the customer feel empowered to choose.

With a $3,250 sale and a customer clearly invested in system health, a brief mention of the SAM membership (which would have included free annual maintenance checks and discount on future repairs) could have added value without changing the close outcome. This is the one gap.
