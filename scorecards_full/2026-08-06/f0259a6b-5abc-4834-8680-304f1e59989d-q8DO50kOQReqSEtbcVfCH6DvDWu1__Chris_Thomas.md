### What Won This Sale
When you showed the burnt voltage switch next to the clean one, then explained "steel on steel...it's going to weld together...that'll make your system run constantly and damage your compressor," the customer was ready. You didn't just diagnose—you connected the finding to the consequence (catastrophic $6k compressor failure) and then immediately moved to options with specific pricing for prevention. That's the sequence.

Specific moment: "Um, so you're pretty much steel on steel at this point" → [Customer: "Yep"] → "Um, that is to the point where very shortly...it's going to weld together" → [Explain system runs 24/7] → [Explain $6,000-8,000 compressor cost] → "Um, so I would definitely suggest replacing that today."

You didn't ask "do you want to do this?" (yes/no binary). You said "if you were to go with those, you'd be looking at $3,200" → manager approval → "I can bring it down to $3,250" → "Okay, let's do it." You made the buy feel inevitable by assuming they'd say yes after you'd built the value.
