The one-line verdict

The moment on BARRETT

She described the product. He confirmed it exists. Nobody wrote it up.

What actually happened

The customer had researched NV Energy&#x27;s thermostat access and refused it twice — that means they can access and that's not what I want. Nathan agreed, explained peak-time control accurately, and added: I always tell people we do carry that same type of thermostat without NV Energy having access to it. Then the conversation moved on.

What it means

She had done the research, formed the objection and stated the requirement. He named the matching product out loud. All that was missing was Would you like me to put that on your report with a price so you can look at it? — one sentence between a want and an option.

What Nathan did right — real strength, build on it

  • <b>He asks where to park before he parks.</b> Small, and it is the first thing every customer notices.
  • <b>He gives away work.</b> Washing the washable filters while he is there, chasing the plumbing department for a booking the customer could not complete online — neither was asked for.
  • <b>He tells customers the truth about their utility.</b> The peak-time control explanation was accurate and he backed the customer&#x27;s own research instead of talking around it.
  • <b>He commits to showing, not telling.</b> I'll come out and grab you guys and show you everything in person is the right instinct — it just needs to survive to the end of the recording.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Write the want
Drill: Any product or upgrade a customer expresses interest in goes on the report with a price, the same visit.
Proof it worked: A report line for every stated want, checked against the recording.
Week 2
Frame the catch-up
Drill: Any system without recent maintenance gets a catch-up plan named before the evaluation starts.
Proof it worked: A plan framing audible ahead of the evaluation on every neglected system.
Week 3
Say the destination
Drill: Before leaving for tools, state what the customer will be shown when he returns.
Proof it worked: A stated next step audible before every tool run.
