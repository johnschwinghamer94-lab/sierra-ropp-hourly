The one-line verdict

Why neither call moved

The right script, twenty minutes late.

What actually happened

Both visits ran diagnosis, history and long personal conversation first, then arrived at so before I get started, I just want to go over this. So this is what you can expect of me — a complete, correct expectations script delivered near the end of the recording.

What it means

The script is what buys the option later, which is why it belongs at the door. Said in the first two minutes it frames everything that follows; said at minute twenty it is a formality, and by then the recording is nearly over.

What David did right — real strength, build on it

  • <b>He asks the power-bill question.</b> What's your guys energy feel like? was skipped by most of the board and it is the question that makes an efficiency conversation possible later.
  • <b>He chases the history rather than guessing at it.</b> Getting the absent husband on the phone mid-visit is more work than most techs will do, and it produced the real service record.
  • <b>He teaches without being asked.</b> The thermostat-swing explanation was volunteered, correct and immediately useful to the customer.
  • <b>He is easy to have in the house.</b> Two long visits, two customers talking freely about family, neighbourhood and money — that is trust doing its job.
