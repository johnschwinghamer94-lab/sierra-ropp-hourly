1

The one-line verdict

Why neither call moved

He suggested the specialist instead of booking one.

What actually happened

On EDWARDS, after showing the burned capacitor wiring and the amp draw running above spec, he closed with I can always get them out here and they can take a look at it.

What it means

I can always is a standing offer, and standing offers do not go on a calendar. The same sentence with a day in it — our specialist is out this way Thursday, morning or afternoon? — is the whole difference between this call and a flip.

What Cole did right — real strength, build on it

  • <b>He reads the customer and changes register.</b> Talking LRA and RLA to an electrician is not showing off — it is the fastest possible route to trust with that particular customer.
  • <b>He finds root causes, not symptoms.</b> The EDWARDS fan-motor burnout was traced back to a previous installer&#x27;s improper power supply, measured against spec rather than asserted.
  • <b>He shows, not tells.</b> The burned capacitor wiring was put in front of the customer; the before-and-after evidence did the arguing for him.
  • <b>The right instinct on price.</b> Replacement pricing was routed to the specialist rather than guessed at, which is exactly the SILO process.

Three-week training plan
