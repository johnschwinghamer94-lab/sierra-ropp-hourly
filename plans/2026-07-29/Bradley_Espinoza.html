The one-line verdict

Why it did not move

Perfect opening, unanswered objection.

What actually happened

The customer explained that her husband had died, that his was the larger cheque, and I cannot get the payment plan because I cannot afford it. Bradley answered with genuine sympathy — I'm sorry to hear that — and when she concluded I'll stay until it dropped, he let that stand and went back to the inspection.

What it means

Empathy without a question is only half the model. The FSG sequence is empathy, then a question, then a redirect to helping — and the help here costs her nothing: a no-cost specialist visit so she knows the number before the system fails in August, not after.

5

What Bradley did right — real strength, build on it

  • <b>His Welcome script is the standard.</b> Tour, thorough evaluation, cleanliness and functionality and safety, permission to raise findings, nothing without approval, five-star commitment — all of it, in order, at the door.
  • <b>He builds real rapport, not filler.</b> Heritage, birds, the orchids, her late husband&#x27;s garden — all customer-led, none of it forced, and she was visibly at ease with him in the house.
  • <b>He asks permission constantly.</b> You mind if I come in?, Do you mind if I take a photo of it?, You mind if I turn it down a bit just to get it going?
  • <b>He is honest about the system without frightening her.</b> Nine years, close to the end of its cycle — stated plainly, without a scare campaign.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Empathy, then question
Drill: Every objection gets a question after the empathy, before any solution: acknowledge, ask, then help.
Proof it worked: A question audible after every objection on tape, not a pivot.
Week 2
The no-cost path
Drill: Every budget-constrained customer hears the no-cost specialist visit offered as information, not as a sale.
Proof it worked: An offered — and ideally dated — specialist visit on every call where money comes up.
Week 3
Ask the maintenance question
Drill: What were you hoping we could accomplish while I'm here for your maintenance? on every tune-up, at the door.
Proof it worked: The question audible on every maintenance recording.
