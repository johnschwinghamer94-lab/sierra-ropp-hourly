The one-line verdict

Why ALCONCEL flipped

He protected her money before he sold her anything.

What actually happened

When the customer said I don't want no ac, Andrew did not push the repair. He said I'm worried about you spending a thousand dollars and then this compressor could fail... So I did give you the third option for me to schedule for someone to come talk to you about a new air conditioning. When she insisted her husband be there, he found a Saturday or Sunday around their church and seniors-club schedule.

What it means

That is the Option C flip done properly — the customer&#x27;s risk named out loud, the specialist offered as protection rather than as an upsell, and the household&#x27;s decision-making respected instead of worked around. She signed the appointment form.

What Andrew did right — real strength, build on it

  • <b>The Option C flip is textbook.</b> He named the customer&#x27;s financial risk out loud and offered the consultation as protection from it: I'm worried about you spending a thousand dollars and then this compressor could fail.
  • <b>He schedules around the household, not against it.</b> Church on Sunday morning, seniors club during the week — he worked the appointment into the gaps rather than pressing for the soonest slot.
  • <b>He adapts his register to the customer.</b> Plain analogies for the ALCONCEL customer, compressor amps and refrigerant phase behaviour for the retired engineer on JOHNSON.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Script every call
Drill: The full expectations script at the door on every visit regardless of length, including return trips and quick looks.
Proof it worked: An audible expectations statement on every recording, short calls included.
Week 2
Decide in the room
Drill: No visit ends with the option choice going to a callback; the customer picks a path or books a specialist before the van door shuts.
Proof it worked: Every long call ending in either a named option or a booked appointment.
Week 3
Ask for the five
Drill: Review ask in the doorway on every call.
Proof it worked: A review request audible on every recording.
