The one-line verdict

Why it flipped

What actually happened

They banned that 2020... the government only allows us to use whatever we have left on our shelf. They don't allow us to import it or buy it anymore — then the system&#x27;s age put in the customer&#x27;s own terms: This house that was built in 99, we're in 2026... You're basically going on its second life right now. It usually lasts about 12 to 15 years.

What it means

That is a reframe, not an argument. The customer&#x27;s position was if it still works, I am not replacing it and Francisco never contradicted it — he changed what works means: inefficient, noisy, and dependent on a refrigerant nobody can buy. The specialist went out on the back of that.

5

What Francisco did right — real strength, build on it

  • <b>He turns readings into meaning.</b> Twenty-four degrees against a floor of thirty-two is a number; that's below cooling is a diagnosis the customer can repeat back.
  • <b>He uses the phase-out honestly.</b> No scare campaign — just the plain fact that the refrigerant cannot be bought any more and what that does to the price of a repair.
  • <b>He answers the fear behind the question.</b> Twenty-four-seven availability was volunteered before the customer had to ask what happens when it fails on a Sunday.
  • <b>He kept the price where it belongs.</b> The replacement number went to the specialist, with the ladder left up so the measurements could be taken — the process working exactly as intended.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Time the specialist
Drill: Every specialist handoff leaves with a confirmed day and window, not an ETA to follow.
Proof it worked: A confirmed appointment window audible on every flip.
Week 2
Ask on the long calls
Drill: No visit over an hour ends without a direct ask for the next step.
Proof it worked: A this-week-or-next question on every long recording.
Week 3
Script at the door
Drill: Approval line and five-star commitment on every call, including return visits.
Proof it worked: Both lines audible in the opening minutes of every recording.
