1

The one-line verdict

Why four flipped

He tells people what cannot be bought any more, and lets that do the arguing.

What he said

This refrigerant was banned in 2025, so getting these components, unless the system's still under warranty, is not possible. And to a home-warranty household: the smaller companies that work with warranty firms do things not the legal way… we get fined, I lose my license. And to a customer weighing a four-thousand-dollar compressor on a twenty-year-old unit: you want to burn a lot of money into an older system?

Why it works

None of it is pressure. It is a supply fact, a licensing fact and an arithmetic question, and each one ends in the same place — a free replacement number from someone whose job that is. He never claims to know what a new system costs, which is exactly why customers believe him when he says someone else should tell them.
