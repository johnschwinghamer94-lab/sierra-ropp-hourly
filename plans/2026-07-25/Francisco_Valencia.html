1

The one-line verdict

Why all four flipped

He sends the specialist while he is still writing the report.

What he said

The other guy can come out while I'm writing the report. That way you can get both options laid out and then make a decision. My role is just to educate you on the repairs… those guys are going to come out and give you the more permanent solution. And on the ninety-two-minute visit: the capacitor tonight, the advisor Wednesday between eight and ten, confirmed with the customer before he left.

Why it works

He separates his job from the advisor's job explicitly, which removes every reason for a customer to feel sold to. Then he overlaps the two visits so the customer never has to make a second decision to get the second number. That is the whole method, and it worked four times out of four.
