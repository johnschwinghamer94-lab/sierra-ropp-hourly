4

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • <b>He asks the power-bill follow-up.</b> How much are you guys paying per month when it comes to keeping it that low? — the single most-skipped required question on this board, and he asks it naturally.
  • <b>Membership handled with real specifics.</b> Named the benefit, the visit count and the signup month. That is what turns a membership from a line item into a relationship.
  • <b>Disarmed real distrust.</b> That's super valid followed by I'll make sure to get approval from you before I do anything. Because the last thing I want to do is give you a surprise. A customer who had just been ripped off by a locksmith relaxed within one exchange.
  • <b>Checks the decision-maker early.</b> Is there anyone else other than you that will want to have that information? — asked before any findings, which is exactly when it is useful.

The fix Andrew's intake is a model. His check-ins, presentation, objection handling and close are entirely invisible to us, on both calls, on the same day. Start every visit with a recorder check and do not stop it until you are back in the van. Until that changes, his close rate on this page is a measurement artifact and nothing more.

Weak

Gap 2 — The five-star seed is never planted

Andrew's expectation block, GLYNN"I'll make sure if there are any concerns with the system performance wise, what's coming down, what we need to address. I'll bring that up with you. We'll go over it together."
What is missingNo mention of the review, the five-star goal, or the standard he is holding himself to — on either call.

The fix The five-star seed is the last beat of the expectations block and it costs one sentence: My goal today is that you'd give this a five-star experience — if I'm falling short of that at any point, stop me. Planted at the front, the ask at the end stops feeling like a favour and starts feeling like the natural end of the visit.

Weak

Gap 3 — Parking and floor protection go unaddressed

What he does ask"Where do you feel like would be the best place to set up my ladder?"
What he never asksWhere the truck should sit, and whether floor protection is going down before he walks through the house.

The fix He clearly has the instinct — the ladder question proves it. Extend it two sentences earlier: confirm the truck is parked where they want it before you knock, and put the protectors down where they can see you do it. On the 1946 house especially, that is the visible signal that you respect their home.

This week's drill — close the expectations block

The one sentence he is missing

<b>He already says:</b> I'll check out the full system... I'll make sure to get approval from you before I do anything.

<b>Add the last beat, every time:</b>

"And my goal today is that this is a five-star experience for you. If I'm falling short of that at any point, stop me on the spot."

Say it before the tools come out. Then the review ask at the end is just keeping a promise.
