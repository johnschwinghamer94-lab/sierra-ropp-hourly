5

What Andrew did right — real strength, build on it

  • He asks what the household pays every month. Almost nobody on this board does, and it is the whole efficiency argument in one question.
  • He asks who else needs to be in the room before he does the work, which is how absent-spouse endings get prevented.
  • He asks what the customer wants out of the visit and gets a real answer: honesty, and no repeat of the company that tried to replace the units for no reason.
  • He is trusted quickly by customers who have been burned, which is the hardest thing on this list to teach.

Three-week training plan

Proof it worked: Every recording reaches the end of the visit.
