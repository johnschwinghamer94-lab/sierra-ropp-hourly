The one-line verdict

Why neither flipped

He offers the specialist as a condition and withdraws it at the first sign of hesitation.

What he said

So I put this option just if you want to speak with the equipment specialist so we can get them out here so you can go over all your options. The customer: I am planning to get a couple other quotes, so I think I'll just kind of think about it. David: Okay, sounds good.

What should follow

A customer collecting quotes has just told you they intend to buy. "Then let's make sure ours is one of them — it's free and it takes an hour. What day are the others coming?" On the other call the closing offer was a portable air conditioner from a hardware shop; that is a kindness, and it is not an option.

5

What David did right — real strength, build on it

  • The best technical explanation on the board. The contactor analogy and the locked-rotor walkthrough are training material as they stand.
  • He states the approval promise and the timeline and keeps both, on both calls.
  • He is honest about part availability rather than promising a date he cannot hold.
  • He took signatures on the diagnostic work and left the customer with everything in writing.

The three gaps

each one in David's own calls
Weak

Gap 1 — the customer announced they were shopping and the offer was withdrawn

CustomerI am planning to get a couple other quotes, so I think I'll just kind of think about it.
DavidOkay, sounds good.

The fix That sentence means they are buying, from someone. "Then ours should be on the list — it's free, it takes an hour, and you'll have it before the others arrive. What day?" Nothing about that is pushy and it is the difference between this day scoring zero and scoring well.

Weak

Gap 2 — the closing offer was a hardware-shop portable unit

David, to a family with two small children in a heatwaveYou can go to, like, Home Depot and get one. Bedroom can stay cool just to get you…
What was not offeredA replacement number, an advisor, or a date.

The fix It is a genuinely kind instinct and it filled the space where the offer belonged. Say both: "Get a portable for the bedroom tonight — and let me get our advisor here tomorrow at no cost so you're not living like this next week."

Moderate

Gap 3 — three hours of tape with no goal question and no bill question

Asked across both callsOnset, symptoms, prior work.
Not askedWhat would make today a win, what the household pays monthly, how long they plan to stay.

The fix Each is one sentence and each changes what the presentation can do. Put all three on a dash card next to the approval promise he never forgets.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Answer the shopping signal
Drill: Any mention of other quotes is met with a proposed date for ours.
Proof it worked: A date offered every time a competitor is named.
Week 2
Three questions in the welcome
Drill: Goal, monthly bill and stay-length asked before the tools come out.
Proof it worked: All three on every recording.
Week 3
Offer, then comfort
Drill: Any interim-comfort advice comes after the offer, never instead of it.
Proof it worked: An advisor offer on every no-cool call.
8

What we owe David Canales

  • Use his teaching for training. The technical explanation is the best here and should be recorded deliberately.
  • Objection drilling. Two clean objections went unanswered on the same day; that is a rehearsal problem, not a character problem.
  • A shorter route to the report. Two visits past a hundred minutes with no decision means the structure, not the effort, is the constraint.
Bottom line

Three hundred minutes of genuinely excellent teaching, and both households were left to make the next move themselves. One of them told him outright they were about to buy. The whole plan is learning to hear that sentence and answer it with a date.
