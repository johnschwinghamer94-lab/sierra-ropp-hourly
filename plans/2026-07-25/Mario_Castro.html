The one-line verdict

What the tape shows

He asks the questions nobody else asks — and then the tape ends.

What actually happened

On the Spanish call he led with the bill: ¿ha visto que sus pagos de electricidad han subido? When the customer said he did not understand the connection, Mario offered to draw it: ¿Cómo se lo explico? Tiene como un pedazo de papel, ahí se lo dibujo — then taught startup amperage and named the real numbers people pay: hay personas que pagan como más de 300, 400, 500 dólares al mes. Se les hace normal pero no saben que el sistema es la causa.

What the FSG model calls for

That is the replacement conversation being built correctly, from the customer's own bill outward. Nothing to fix in it. What we cannot see, on any of three calls, is what he did with it — every recording stops before the first finding is delivered.

Plant it before the tools come out and the ask at the end writes itself.

<b>At the end of every expectations block, in either language:</b>
