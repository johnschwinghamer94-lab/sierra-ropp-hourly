The one-line verdict

Why the day scores as it does

The conversions are real and invisible.

What is on tape

A clean, curious welcome: the damper history, who adjusted the thermostat settings, which rooms get no airflow, how long they have lived there, and the life expectancy of builder-grade equipment in this climate — usually they're original to the home, they usually last eight to ten years. On the maintenance call, the time-constraint question, asked properly.

What is not on tape

Everything after the welcome, on two of three calls. One recording spends its final third on a conversation between technicians about a fleet-software write-up, recorded over what should have been a customer visit. The lead in ServiceTitan came from a call whose decision moment does not exist on tape.
