1

The one-line verdict

Why it flipped

He kept the repair alive as a fallback and that is why the replacement conversation felt safe.

What he said

We can always circle back and be out here in the morning on the repairs — said immediately after offering the replacement conversation, not instead of it. Then: would you want to get something scheduled to have that conversation with? and the signature.

Why it works

A customer who believes the repair door has closed behind them stalls. A customer who knows both doors are open picks one. Cole gave them the replacement route and left the repair route standing, then took a signature and stayed on site to hand over in person.

5

What Cole did right — real strength, build on it

  • A complete visit on tape — welcome, diagnosis, report, presentation, objection, signature and handover. Nobody else produced a recording this complete this day.
  • He kept the repair option alive while selling the replacement conversation. That is the single most transferable move in this whole regrade period.
  • He stayed on site to brief the advisor so the customer never had to explain the problem twice.
  • He made the compressor fault arithmetic rather than opinion, which is why it was not argued with.
