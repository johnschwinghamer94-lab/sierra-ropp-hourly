The one-line verdict

The moment that matters

He made the offer. He never set the date.

What actually happened

When the customer said he had to check the month's other bills first — quiero ver qué otras cosas tiene ella que tenemos que pagar porque ya se viene el primero, ya ves la luz, agua — Juan responded well: payments — todo se puede hacer en pagos — and then the specialist: si quieres también puedo hacer el schedule para que venga y te hable alguien del precio. The customer answered by saying he would talk to his wife and call back: voy a platicar con ella, entonces ahí te marco... yo pienso que ya para el domingo.

What the FSG model calls for

Si quieres makes the specialist visit something the customer has to want. It is free and it costs him nothing, so it should be proposed with a time already attached — Te lo agendo para mañana en la mañana, no cuesta nada y así ya tienen el número cuando platiquen — and then silence. The customer never said no; he was never actually asked.

6

This week's drill — agendar, no ofrecer

Schedule it, don't offer it

<b>Trigger:</b> tengo que ver, voy a platicar con mi esposa, ahí te marco.

<b>Response, right away:</b>

"Perfecto — y para que platiquen con el número enfrente, te agendo al especialista. No cuesta nada, no hay compromiso. ¿Mañana en la mañana o en la tarde?"

Y ahí te callas. The customer picks a time or he doesn't — but he has to be asked before he can say yes.

7

3-week training plan

Week 1
The three required questions
Filter, rooms, and summer-winter temperature plus the power bill — at the thermostat, every call, in Spanish or English.
Week 2
Schedule, don't offer
Every specialist mention converts to a proposed day and time. Manager reviews three calls for offers versus bookings.
Week 3
Nothing leaves unscheduled
Repair date, specialist appointment or a specific callback time on every single visit. Track it.

Bottom line

Juan sets expectations properly, keeps the pressure off, and reaches for the right third option without being told to. What he does not do is ask. The customer never refused the specialist visit — he was offered it as something he might want, changed the subject, and left to talk to his wife with no number and nothing booked. Propose a day and a time instead of asking whether he'd like one, and this call ends differently.
