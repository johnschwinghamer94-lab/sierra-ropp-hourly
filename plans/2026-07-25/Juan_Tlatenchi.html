1

The one-line verdict

Why it did not flip

An offer that starts with if you want puts the work of asking on the customer.

What he said

He raised payments so the customer could arreglar tu financial, then offered to schedule someone to quote the replacement so the customer could weigh a twenty-two-hundred-dollar motor against a whole new system. The customer replied that he would talk to his wife and call back — ya para el domingo ya nos damos cuenta.

What should follow

Sunday is not a commitment; it is a way of ending a conversation politely. "Perfecto — y mientras hablas con ella, dejo agendado al especialista para el martes. No cuesta nada y si no les sirve, lo cancelamos." The appointment costs the customer nothing and gives the household a real number to discuss instead of a remembered one.

5

What Juan did right — real strength, build on it

  • He raised financing before the customer had to ask, which is the right move on a repair of that size.
  • He did offer the price appointment. Several reps on this board never mention it at all.
  • The whole conversation is in the customer's language and loses no technical content for it.
  • He took the diagnostic authorisations properly and explained what each signature meant.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Book, don't offer
Drill: Every specialist mention ends with a date on the calendar and an easy way to cancel.
Proof it worked: An appointment set on every call where replacement is discussed.
Week 2
Record the whole visit
Drill: Recorder confirmed at the door and checked before the van door closes.
Proof it worked: A recording that starts with the greeting and ends with the goodbye.
Week 3
The welcome, said aloud
Drill: Approval promise, five-star framing and the goal question, in the first two minutes.
Proof it worked: All three audible on every recording.
8

What we owe Juan Tlatenchi

  • A cancellable-appointment script in Spanish. He has the offer and needs the closing form of it.
  • Recorder support. Two graded days of fragmentary tape is a process problem before it is a performance one.
  • Credit for the financing instinct — raising payments unprompted is right and it should stay.
Bottom line

The offer existed and was phrased as a favour, so it was declined politely and nothing was booked. Book the appointment, make cancelling easy, and record the visit that produced it.
