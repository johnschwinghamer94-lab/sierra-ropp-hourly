1

The one-line verdict

What the tape shows

He brings the customer into the work instead of reporting back to her.

What actually happened

Twice he handed the diagnosis over physically. Or if you'd like, we can do it together. I can show you everything that's going on while we go. Then, at the condenser: if you want to, you can kind of give it a tap and if you want to get a little closer with me, you can as well. The customer went from what do you call that thing? to following the burn marks on a contactor.

What the FSG model calls for

That instinct is the model's hardest behavior arriving naturally. What is missing is the frame around it: he never promised approval before work, never committed to today, and never planted the five-star seed. The teaching is already there — the expectations block that makes the teaching pay off is not.

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • <b>He explains why he is asking.</b> typically if the power bill is going up, that could be a sign that the system is working harder than it should — the customer answers a better question when she knows what it is for.
  • <b>Invited her into the diagnosis physically.</b> if you want to, you can kind of give it a tap / if you want to get a little closer with me, you can as well.
  • <b>Separated urgent from upcoming, honestly.</b> right now that's not the thing that's preventing you from cooling — he could have bundled it and did not.
  • <b>Re-asked a question that did not land.</b> When the customer misheard the power-bill question, he rephrased it instead of moving on. That is patience most reps do not have.

The three gaps

each one anchored to a real moment on tape
Weak

Gap 1 — No approval promise, no today, no five-star

What he said"I'm gonna find out what's going on... and then we'll sit down together and go over every option for you."
What was never said"I won't do anything without your approval" · "my goal is to take care of this today" · "my goal is a five-star experience."

The fix Two of six beats is not an expectations block. The approval sentence is what makes an expensive finding safe to hear, and the today commitment is what makes a repair feel solvable rather than scheduled. Add all four missing beats to the rundown he already gives — it costs about fifteen seconds and it changes how everything after it is received.

Weak

Gap 2 — The tape stops before anything is presented

Last words on tape"Just running a couple more tests for you really quick."

The fix His check-in work is genuinely excellent and it is the only thing we can grade. Confirm the recorder before the knock and leave it running through the presentation and the goodbye. On a rep this good at bringing a customer along, the presentation is the part we most need to see.

Customer, unprompted"I set it up to 78... today I set it up to 76."
What was never asked"What do you keep it set at in the summer, and what about the winter?"

The fix She volunteered a number in the middle of a story about her neighbour's advice — that is not the same as the required question, and it left the winter setting and the seasonal pattern unknown. Ask it deliberately at the thermostat. The customer had already closed off two bedrooms to save money; the full question would have opened that conversation properly.

6

This week's drill — the four sentences he is missing

Say all six beats, every call

<b>He already says:</b> I'm gonna find out what's going on... then we'll sit down together and go over every option.

<b>Add, in the same breath:</b>

"I'll look at the whole system, not just this one thing, and I'll do a full evaluation. I won't do anything without your approval first, and my goal is to take care of everything today if we can. And I'm going for a five-star experience — if I'm falling short at any point, stop me."

Then go to work. Everything he already does well lands harder once this is said.
