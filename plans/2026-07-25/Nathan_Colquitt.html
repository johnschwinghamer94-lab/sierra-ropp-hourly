1

The one-line verdict

Why the day scores as it does

The best-diagnosed call of the day has no ending.

What is on tape

Three faults found and explained in sequence: a burning contactor shown up close, a fan motor that comes to an abrupt stop when spun, and a capacitor reading well under its rating. Each one demonstrated physically, each one explained without jargon, each one framed as part of a combination rather than as the single cause.

What is missing

Everything after the third fault. A household with a burnt contactor, a seized motor and a weak capacitor on the same system is the clearest replacement conversation on this day's board, and there is no record of it being had. On the evidence available this is a recording failure, not a selling failure — but it is now the sixth graded day in a row it has happened.
