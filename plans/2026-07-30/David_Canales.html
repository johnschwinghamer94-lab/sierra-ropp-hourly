The one-line verdict

The room that stayed hot

He signed the membership and left the actual complaint unsolved.

What actually happened

The customer raised the same thing more than once: one upstairs room gets too hot in summer and does not cool well. David acknowledged it — Okay, so it gets hot. What about wintertime? — checked it during the inspection, and reported that everything checks out.

What it means

The membership was a genuine flip and the right sale. But the customer's own stated problem never got a cause or a fix. One follow-up — is it a duct run, a damper, a return-air problem? — would have turned a satisfied customer into a second, larger opportunity, and would have made the membership feel like a solution rather than a subscription.

What David did right — real strength, build on it

  • He makes customers feel genuinely heard. The rapport is not technique — it is real interest, and it is why a maintenance visit turned into a recurring relationship.
  • He sells the membership on the right axis. Not price, not discounts — that takes that thing out of your mind. For a driver who is out on the road for weeks, that is the exact benefit that matters.
  • He creates work for other departments. The water heater flush agreement is a plumbing lead nobody asked him to generate.
  • He states the standard early. I'm here for a 5-star experience is said before he touches anything, which sets the tone for the whole visit.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Solve the stated complaint
Drill: Whatever the customer names as their problem gets a cause or a written next step before the visit ends — no exceptions.
Proof it worked: Every customer-stated complaint traceable to a finding or a report line.
Week 2
Diagnosis first, stories alongside
Drill: Reach the diagnostic work early and carry the conversation while the tools are moving.
Proof it worked: Diagnosis underway within the first third of every visit.
Week 3
Collect at the peak
Drill: The review ask goes in immediately after payment or signature.
Proof it worked: A five-star request audible on every completed visit.
