The one-line verdict

The pivot on JIMENEZ

Six thousand seven hundred dollars, refused, and he still left with two yeses.

What actually happened

The evidence was overwhelming and he had built it properly — compressor at 21.47 amps against a 26 maximum, dropping to 11 after cleaning and climbing back to 18.4, locked rotor amperage at 140 against a 134 limit, an outdoor unit never cleaned in nine years, and a restricted metering device icing the line. Then the price landed and the customer said no.

What it means

He did not discount and he did not argue. I know it sounds like a lot — then the free path: I can get someone out here to go over that information with you. The customer left with a membership and a comfort advisor appointment. That is the Option C move executed under real pressure.

What Joe did right — real strength, build on it

  • He works in the customer's language. Not just Spanish — the whole JIMENEZ visit, technical detail included, delivered so the customer could actually follow the argument.
  • He shows the number twice. The compressor amps before cleaning, after cleaning, and then climbing back — that sequence is what proved internal damage rather than dirt, and the customer could see it happen.
  • He absorbs a refusal without discounting. $6,700 refused, and the answer was a free consultation, not a lower price. That protects the margin and the relationship at the same time.
  • His expectations frame is unusually honest. My goal here is to give you a clean bill of health and say, hey, see you next year tells the customer he is not there to find something, which is why they believe him when he does.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
An option on every call
Drill: Every visit leaves at least one written, priced path — including on rentals, where the renter carries it to the owner.
Proof it worked: At least one option on the report for 100% of calls.
Week 2
Date the 'not ready'
Drill: Any customer who declines gets a specific re-check or consultation date offered before he leaves.
Proof it worked: A named date offered after every decline.
Week 3
Ask on the wins
Drill: Every flip, membership or completed repair ends with an explicit five-star request.
Proof it worked: A review ask audible on every closed call.
