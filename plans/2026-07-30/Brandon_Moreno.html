What actually happened

On ZHANG he correctly identified that a three-zone system works roughly twice as hard as a single-zone and told the customer to expect 13 to 15 years at least. But with the zones, it's just extra. On CADEAUX-EUSTIS he found a high-pressure lockout from failed fans. On HERAODA he caught a compressor warranty about to expire. Three real findings, three houses, no priced option in any of them.

What it means

5

What Brandon did right — real strength, build on it

  • Nobody sets expectations more reliably. Time, approval, no surprises — on every single call, including the twenty-minute one. That is the hardest habit to build and he already has it.
  • He explains wear honestly. The three-zone system working double is exactly the kind of specific, checkable claim that makes a customer believe the next thing you say.
  • He catches things that pay the customer back. The HERAODA compressor warranty expiry is money in the customer's pocket that only a careful tech would have found.
  • He asks before he enters. Small, and it is the first thing a customer in the middle of moving house notices.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
A number on every finding
Drill: Every diagnosis leaves behind a written, priced line on the report before he departs.
Proof it worked: At least one priced option on the report for 100% of calls.
Week 2
Plan the replacement early
Drill: Any system past ten years or under unusual load gets a no-cost specialist visit offered as planning, not as a sale.
Proof it worked: A specialist offer audible on every aging or high-load system.
Week 3
Reconnect on time pushback
Drill: Any pushback on duration gets answered with what the customer receives, not with reassurance.
Proof it worked: A value reconnect audible after every time objection.
