What Cole did right — real strength, build on it

  • He does the Option C handoff exactly right. Manager called, specialist dispatched the same evening, consent stripped to Zero dollar amount. That is the primary win in the SILO model and he executed it cleanly.
  • His diagnostic opening is a template. Names, water, permission, scope, report process, approval boundary, five-star promise — all inside the first two minutes on LIAN.
  • He asks the severity question. Are you having that issue on both the second and the first floor, or is it just the first? is the question that separates a zoning problem from a system problem, and most techs skip it.
  • He does not try to answer the pricing question himself. Routing replacement cost to the specialist is the process working as designed, and it is why COTE flipped.
