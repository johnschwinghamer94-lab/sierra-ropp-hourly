What Nathan did right — real strength, build on it

  • He shows rather than tells. The in-person component demonstration on KILAND is the single behaviour that separates his flip from his no-closes, and it is entirely repeatable.
  • He compares costs honestly. Putting the paths side by side lets the customer decide rather than be steered, which is why the KILAND close held.
  • He is genuinely warm. Sympathy for a family returning to a hot house, humour with the BRAVO customers, shared personal ground — nobody minds having him in their home.
  • He asks before he moves through the house. Do you mind if I see the outdoor as well? Small, and it is the difference between a guest and an intruder.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Book the stated timeline
Drill: Any customer who names a future buying window gets a no-cost specialist offer inside sixty seconds.
Proof it worked: A specialist offer audible after every stated timeline.
Week 2
Summarise before you promise
Drill: Every visit ends with a spoken summary of the findings before any mention of a written report.
Proof it worked: A spoken findings summary audible at the end of every call.
Week 3
KILAND treatment everywhere
Drill: Components shown in person on every call, regardless of length or apparent stakes.
Proof it worked: An in-person demonstration on every visit that reaches the equipment.
