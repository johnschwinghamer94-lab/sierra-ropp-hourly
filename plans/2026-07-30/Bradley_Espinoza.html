What Bradley did right — real strength, build on it

  • He argues against his own invoice when it is right. Telling an eight-year-system owner not to over-invest is the single most trust-building sentence anyone said on tape all day.
  • His expectations frame is the best on the board when he uses it. Tour, evaluation, van, report, approval — stated in order, with permission asked before flagging anything.
  • He asks permission rather than assuming it. Is it okay that I bring it up to your attention? converts every later finding from an upsell into something she invited.
  • He explains rather than asserts. Locked rotor amperage defined in one plain sentence, so the reading meant something to her instead of just sounding serious.
