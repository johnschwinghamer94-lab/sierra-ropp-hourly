5

What Andrew did right — real strength, build on it

  • He prices honestly and never apologises for the number. $6,700 for the TXV repair, $8,000-plus for replacement, said plainly, then explained: R22 at $600-plus a pound and nine pounds needed. The customer argued with the situation, not with him.
  • He reads a hard start device as a story, not a part. Recognising it as a previous owner's band-aid on a stressed compressor is what let him explain why the system is on borrowed time without sounding alarmist.
  • He converts refusal into an appointment. I can get someone out here — offered the moment the customer drew a spending line, not five minutes later after more persuading.
  • He respects a time constraint and still closes. The earlier customer had afternoon meetings and was leaving Sunday. He honoured it, did not oversell, and still got the authorisation signed.
