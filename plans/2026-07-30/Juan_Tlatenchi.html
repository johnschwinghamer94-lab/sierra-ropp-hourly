5

What Juan did right — real strength, build on it

  • His measurements do the arguing. 0.9 against a specification of 70 is not an opinion — it is a fact a customer can check, and it makes everything after it credible.
  • He traces consequences, not just faults. Bad start device to labouring compressor to a dirty filter adding load — a chain, not a list. That is how a customer comes to understand their own system.
  • He finds the second problem. Most techs stop at the headline fault. Separating the filter as an independent load driver is careful work.
  • He read the LAW situation honestly. A household mid-sale with an inspection the next day is not a sales opportunity, and he did not manufacture one. That is professional judgement, not a failure.
