The one-line verdict

The customer asked him what to do first. He said he would break it down, and then did not.

What actually happened

Four serious findings in one house — water in the pan, your last resort before it spills over onto your ceiling, a corroding coil, a heat exchanger where not if it cracks but when it cracks, and a capacitor at 33 that can't go below 38. 5

What Mario did right — real strength, build on it

  • He makes failure physical. Water in the pan as your last resort before it spills over onto your ceiling, rust as the same thing that happens to cars, metal fatigue as a paperclip bent back and forth. Customers remember pictures, not part names.
  • He shows people their own house. The OTTESON customer had never been in his attic. Photographs of the rusted coil and the oil-tracked motors turned an abstract fault into his property.
  • He works the late calls properly. Arriving after dark to a ninety-degree house and still running the full expectations script, including the offer to start the work that night, is discipline.
  • He states urgency without melodrama. not if it cracks but when it cracks is accurate, memorable and does not overstate the case.
