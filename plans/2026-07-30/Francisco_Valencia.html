The one-line verdict

The sentence that flipped JIAO

He made the maintenance plan about her breakdown, not about maintenance.

What actually happened

The customer was worried about waiting six months without cover. Francisco answered: if we were able to get a maintenance, we would have noticed that that capacitor was starting to lose charge and recommended changing it before it broke. She replied immediately — Okay, then we'll do the membership.

What it means

That is not preventive maintenance is valuable. It is maintenance would have caught the exact part that failed in your house today. Causality, not category. It is the most transferable sentence anyone said on tape all day, and it should be on the board.

What Francisco did right — real strength, build on it

  • He proves rather than claims. Not your compressor is bad but 145 amps against a 127 rating. Not replacement costs more now but the refrigerant law that made it so. Customers argue with opinions and not with meters.
  • He is unhurried with people who need time. Five rounds of membership clarification across a language barrier, and the tone never changed. That is the reason JIAO flipped.
  • The prevention reframe is his best asset. Tying maintenance to the exact part that failed in that house today is a repeatable, teachable move.
  • He manages expectations after the fix. Explaining why the house will keep running until sundown prevents the callback that would otherwise arrive that evening.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Ask for the decision
Drill: Every call that reaches a full explanation ends with an explicit closing question — move forward, or think about it.
Proof it worked: A closing question audible on every call that reaches the options stage.
Week 2
Offer the membership to loyal customers
Drill: Any customer with multi-year history and no membership gets it presented and priced on the report.
Proof it worked: A membership line on the report for every long-tenure customer without one.
Week 3
Convert the compliment
Drill: Any praise for Sierra's service or reports is answered within a minute with a concrete offer.
Proof it worked: An offer audible after every service compliment.
