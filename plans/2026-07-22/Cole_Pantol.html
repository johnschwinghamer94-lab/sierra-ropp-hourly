What went right — real strength, build on it

  • He solved the decision-maker problem in the first five minutes. Will he be home later, or do you want to give him a call on the phone? — number taken, and the customer offered the option of being on the call too.
  • He ran the full block on a call he could have rushed, in a house that had reached 91 degrees inside.

6

This week's drill — the urgent call gets the same sixty seconds

At the thermostat, before the ladder, even when the house is hot

"Before I get up there — how often are the filters getting changed? What do you normally keep this set at when you're here? What does a summer bill run at that setting? And which rooms give you the most trouble?"

He already runs the longest expectations block on the board on a call he could have rushed. The four questions cost less time than the block does, and on a two-system house with one unit dead they are what turn the findings review into a decision.
