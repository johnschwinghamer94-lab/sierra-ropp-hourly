1

The one-line verdict

Why they flipped

He prices the repair honestly and lets the arithmetic make the case.

What actually happened

He never argued for replacement. He named the repair cost, confirmed the part could arrive same day, and offered both paths at once: I can order the motor and get you guys — see if we can get it by today… the equipment specialist — I can get someone out here in the next 30 to 40 minutes. When the customer weighed fifteen hundred against a ten-year-old system, the conclusion was his own.

Why that works

Two live options, both with real timeframes, and no thumb on the scale. Joe's conversion comes from removing the delay from both paths — the motor today, the specialist in twenty minutes — so the customer is choosing between two solutions rather than between acting and waiting.

5

What Joe did right — real strength, build on it

  • Two leads, both earned by removing delay. Part confirmed same-day, specialist on site in twenty minutes — neither path required the customer to wait.
  • He called the manufacturer mid-visit. I was able to contact Lennox. They said they do have it — that is the difference between an estimate and an answer.
  • He cancelled a visit and charged nothing when the system turned out to be running. Customers remember that longer than a repair.
  • Full bilingual service with the scope explained honestly, including what the promotional special does not cover.
  • The sauce-packet analogy for sealed motor lubrication is the clearest technical explanation anyone gave this day.

8

What we owe Joe Mendoza

  • Credit the twenty-minute dispatch. Specialist on site inside half an hour with a family already in a hotel — that is the response we want to be known for.
  • Recognition for calling the manufacturer mid-visit. Turning a guess into a confirmed timeline is what made both options real.
  • The review ask, made mandatory. Two graded days, none asked.
  • A watch-list play. He identifies future problems well and has no standard next step to attach to them.
Bottom line

Joe converted twice by making both paths real: the part confirmed available today, the specialist here in twenty minutes. He never argued for replacement — he priced the repair honestly and let a customer with a hotel bill do the arithmetic himself. What is still missing is small and repeated: no power bill behind the comparison, no advisor offered on the visits where he found nothing to charge for, and no review asked after a day of genuinely good service.
