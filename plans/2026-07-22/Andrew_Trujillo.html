The one-line verdict

The ninety seconds that exist

Textbook, word for word — and the only record of an entire visit.

What he said

For the year 2000 — with this being so old and the issues that we're seeing here, it's clear that it's running on less efficiency than it was back when it was first manufactured. That means that you're paying more than you should to keep this thing running. And with all this in mind, given how much we have to address the system to get it back up to an optimal standpoint, I've given you the option of talking to someone for free… that way you can compare, see if it makes more sense to update the system, get it up to a standard for today, get you on a 10-year warranty — that way you don't have to worry about calling someone out year after year.

What the customer said

Cool. Let's do it. No hesitation, no objection, no price argument. He had already made the case before the ask.

4

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • He turned age into money without claiming a number. It's running on less efficiency than it was when it was first manufactured. That means you're paying more than you should to keep this thing running.
  • He named what the specialist produces, not what they sell. That way you can compare, see if it makes more sense to update the system.
  • He sold the end of the problem, not the equipment. You don't have to worry about calling someone out year after year.
  • He made the next step free and said so. The only price in the whole fragment is zero.
  • He stopped talking after the ask — and the customer filled the silence with a yes.

This week's drill — the same sentence, with their number in it

Keep the structure, add the evidence

"You told me you keep it at [setting] and July runs about [bill]. For a system this age, that's the efficiency you've already lost — you're paying for it every month whether we repair anything or not. So I've given you the option of talking to someone for free, so you can compare that against what a new system would do to that number. No commitment either way."

He already has the best Option C script on the board. This adds the two facts that make it the customer's argument instead of his.
