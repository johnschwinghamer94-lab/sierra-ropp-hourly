The one-line verdict

The day in one pattern

He fixed last week's question and still has no tape of an answer.

What improved

The power-bill question, asked plainly and in context after new windows went in: you'll notice an energy bill savings… what are your energy bills in the summertime? → I paid only $52 a month. And the best justification for a full evaluation anyone gave this day: we've had where we just came out, one part needs to be replaced, we got it back going — and then two weeks later something else went wrong, and you're like, hey, you were just out here. It almost looks like you don't know what you're doing.

What has not changed

Two systems down in one house, an original homeowner and a warranty to check — and the recording stops with the fan not running and the compressor hot. Across two graded days and five calls, no tape has ever reached a price, an option or a specialist.

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • He asked the power-bill question — the exact gap flagged on his last plan — and used the answer against his own interest: that's not even worth getting solar for.
  • He explained why he evaluates the whole system, in terms of the customer's experience rather than company policy: it almost looks like you don't know what you're doing then — you were just out here.
  • He offered the maintenance as a same-visit option rather than a second appointment, and left the choice with the customer.
  • He checked the warranty because of who the customer was, and told him why: original homeowner, registration, coverage.
  • He complimented the previous installer's work where it was good — the sediment trap, the disconnect — instead of scoring points off it.
  • He gave an honest time estimate to a household with two systems down: hour, hour and a half per unit, just diagnosing.

6

This week's drill — finish the set, then finish the tape

At the thermostat, on every call

"What do you keep it set at in the summer? What does the bill run at that setting? How often are the filters getting changed? And which rooms give you the most trouble? … And above all I'm here to give you a five-star visit — if I'm not, stop me."

He now asks two of those four consistently. The other two, plus the standard, take fifteen seconds — and then the recorder needs to survive to the part where he uses them.
