The one-line verdict

The whole file

A model opening, and then the tape gives up.

What is recorded

All six beats, delivered conversationally rather than recited: So I'll run a thorough evaluation of everything. Do you have any time constraints?… If I happen to notice anything of concern, do you want me to bring it up to your attention?… I'll do the outside, come inside and do the inside portion, and then I'll come and touch base with you… I will have to step outside and just write a report for you, and I can include some options depending on what it is I find. And I don't really work without your approval, so you don't have to worry about any surprise bill. Overall I'm here to deliver a five star experience — if I'm not meeting the expectation, let me know.

What is not

Two systems, one of which was recharged at the last visit with an unresolved question hanging over it — we did a recharge because it was low… I can't remember if it was a leak — and the tape ends before he goes outside. The one thing this customer was owed, an answer about whether that system is leaking, is nowhere in the file.

4

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • He gave the approval promise a reason. I don't really work without your approval, so you don't have to worry about any surprise bill. Most of this board states the rule; he states what it means for the customer.
  • He framed the five-star standard as an invitation to complain, not a request for praise: if I'm not meeting the expectation, let me know.
  • He collected both thermostat settings on a two-system house, unprompted, before touching anything.
  • He turned the filter question into a schedule. Right before the heating season and then right before the cooling season.
  • He picked up an unresolved question from a previous visit — a recharge that might or might not have been a leak — and committed to answering it himself.
  • He asked about time constraints and about how to enter and leave before the customer had to think about either.
