What Robert did right — real strength, build on it

  • He let the complaint run. A customer describing a failed repair, four days of heat and a refund request got heard out with no defensiveness.
  • He takes symptom history properly. When it started, whether the noise is new, what repairs preceded it — the right questions in the right order.
  • He asks how the customer wants him to move through the house rather than assuming, on every recording across three graded days.
  • He kept the customer talking long enough that the whole story came out, which is the only way service recovery can start.
