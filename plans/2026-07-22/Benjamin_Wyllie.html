1

The one-line verdict

Why it flipped

He stopped asking when to follow up and started asking for a signature.

HOWELL — the whole model, executed

Immediates named to the dollar — get all four of those completed, we're looking at $3,844. What questions do you have about that? — futures itemised component by component, and then the third path offered with the reason attached: with all the money that we'd have to put into the system just to get it back and going, I did give you guys the option to speak to an install specialist about what updating the system looks like, so you guys can compare and contrast. Customer: any idea on the cost of a new system? He answered honestly, then booked it: it's something I can just get a guy out here real quick, be here in about 30 to 45 minutes… I'll have you sign something real quick.

What is still missing

He never once asked what a power bill costs — and then told the customer they are overpaying by about 15% on their utility bills every single month. That figure is his, not theirs. And on three calls, two of them ending in a happy customer and one in a completed repair, he did not ask a single person for a review.
