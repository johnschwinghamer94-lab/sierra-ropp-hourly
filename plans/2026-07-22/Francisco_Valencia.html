This week's drill — a date on everything

When a customer says "not right now"

"That's completely fair. So let's put a date on it rather than leaving it open — you're back end of September, so I'll have someone reach out the first week of October with current pricing. And if that fan motor goes before then, call me directly. Sound good?"

He already handles the objection better than almost anyone here. The drill is refusing to end a visit with an intention instead of a date.
