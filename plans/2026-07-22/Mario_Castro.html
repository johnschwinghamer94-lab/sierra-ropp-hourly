4

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • The full required set, on both calls, for the second graded day running. Nobody else on this board has managed it once.
  • He asks the bill with a range attached so it is easy to answer: how much are you paying? Are you over 200, 300?
  • He asks why the thermostat sits where it does, which turns a number into a motive: are you comfortable at 76? → mainly for utility bills.
  • He changed the filters for free and said so before being asked. Do you charge for changing the filters? → I'm already here, ma'am. I'm not gonna charge you for that.
  • He asked what to do with bad news in a form that produced an instruction: what would you like me to do with that information? → Give us a printout.
  • He found the addition with the undersized duct — the one thing on that property most likely to be the actual complaint — by asking rather than by looking.
