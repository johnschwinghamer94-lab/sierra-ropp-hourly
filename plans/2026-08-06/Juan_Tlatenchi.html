5

What Juan did right — real strength, build on it

  • He set expectations without being asked to. Scope, findings, options and the approval line, all in one calm run — on a demand call where most technicians go straight to the equipment.
  • He interrogated the previous repair instead of accepting it. if it wasn’t installed properly, it would have given out pretty quick. That one line reframed a year-old Craigslist blower job from “probably the same thing again” to “probably not,” and it was reasoning, not guessing.
  • He would not diagnose before measuring. might not be a capacitor, but I’ll [check] to a customer actively fishing for a cheap answer her husband could fit. Refusing the easy yes is what makes the eventual finding credible.
  • He explained a blower so a homeowner could repeat it. Housing, wheel, motor, air — four nouns, no jargon.
  • He sold the membership on its contents. Warranty extension, two visits, coil clean, gas adjustment, waived emergency fee, plumbing inspection — delivered as a list of what she gets rather than as a monthly charge.
