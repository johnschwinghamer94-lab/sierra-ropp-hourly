What Bradley did right — real strength, build on it

  • He earns two hours in someone's home. Both customers stayed engaged across a hundred-plus minutes, which is a rapport result most techs cannot produce.
  • He notices the person. Recognising military service and meaning it, and finding the car in the driveway interesting because it is — that is the difference between a visit and a transaction.
  • He can present when he chooses to. The VANCLEEF call proves the upgrade conversation is well within his range; it converted on the first attempt.
  • He inspects properly. Neither call was short-changed on the technical work, and the condition summaries were honest.

Gap 1 — 104 minutes on WOODS and no option at the end

What was thereA full maintenance inspection, system age and condition explained, a family who liked him.
What was missingNo upgrade path, no replacement path, no specialist offer, no estimate.

The fix The bridge is one sentence at the end of the inspection. Your system's holding up for its age, but with the kids and how you use the house, let me get you a no-cost number on what a two-stage would do to that bill. Worth seeing? The rapport is already paid for; spend it.
