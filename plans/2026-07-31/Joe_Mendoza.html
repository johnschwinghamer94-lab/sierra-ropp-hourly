5

What Joe did right — real strength, build on it

  • He makes the specialist free before anyone asks the price. It doesn't cost you anything to get the information turned a flat refusal into a signature on the PRICE call, and the same move carried JIMENEZ and Riza.
  • He will talk himself out of money. On Riza he declined the $4,200 clean because it would not fix the fault — I gotta be able to sleep at night... Knowing that you're going to call me back before summer ends. That is why the replacement conversation was welcome.
  • He proves it on camera. Burnt compressor wires, the rusted outdoor coil, the clogged indoor coil — shown, not described, on both long calls.
  • He works in the customer's language. The JIMENEZ call ran in Spanish and English with no loss of technical precision, and a $6,700 objection still landed on Option C.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Diagnosis is never the last sentence
Drill: Every call that names a fault gets an option or an appointment in the next sixty seconds, including the twenty-minute ones.
Proof it worked: No recording ends on a diagnosis with no next step.
Week 2
Book while you recommend
Drill: The specialist recommendation and the day-and-time offer come out as one sentence.
Proof it worked: Every Option C mention is followed by a proposed time on the same recording.
Week 3
Ask for the five stars
Drill: The review ask goes at the moment the appointment is confirmed, on every call.
Proof it worked: An audible review ask on every visit for the week.
