The one-line verdict

The moment on the maintenance visit

He showed the risk, then waived it away.

What actually happened

He had already said it plainly: You will have water damage. You'll have a giant hole in your ceiling if something happens. Then the customer asked do I even do anything with that primary? Do we even waste the money on that? and David answered I'd have to look up the pricing, but yeah. So it's completely up to you. Your house, your money.

What flips it

That question is not a refusal, it is a request for a recommendation. Either quantify the risk between now and the winter replacement, or offer the smaller interim step — we can clear both drains today so you're covered until the new system goes in. Respect for the customer's autonomy is not the same as withholding your opinion.

5

What David did right — real strength, build on it

  • He proves it with instruments. Megohmmeter readings on the compressor windings gave the replacement conversation a foundation no amount of persuasion would have built.
  • He teaches the whole system, not the part. Primary versus secondary versus emergency drains, how condensate moves, why oil loss ends in a seized motor — customers leave able to explain their own equipment.
  • He states risk in physical terms. You'll have a giant hole in your ceiling is exactly the right register: concrete, unarguable, not alarmist.
  • He sets the five-star standard at the door. Naming the standard up front on both calls is a habit most of the board does not have.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Answer the ask
Drill: Any customer question containing should I or is it worth it gets a recommendation before it gets an autonomy statement.
Proof it worked: No recording where a direct request for advice is answered only with it's up to you.
Week 2
Turn the timeline into an appointment
Drill: Every customer who names a future replacement window gets a no-cost specialist offer in the same conversation.
Proof it worked: A specialist offer audible after every stated timeline.
Week 3
Collect the five stars
Drill: The standard set at the door gets referenced and cashed at the end of every visit.
Proof it worked: An audible review ask on every call for the week.
