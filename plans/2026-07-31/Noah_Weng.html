What Noah did right — real strength, build on it

  • He follows the price rule correctly. Deferring replacement pricing to the specialist is the SILO process working exactly as designed, and he did it without hesitating.
  • He states the approval boundary out loud. I'm not going to do anything without you is the sentence that makes a customer comfortable letting you work, and it opens both calls.
  • His discovery is comprehensive. Thermostat settings, bills, room-by-room performance and filter habits is a complete picture, not a checklist.
  • He proves the fault visually. Holding the burnt voltage switch next to a normal one settles the question faster than any explanation.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
The sentence after the diagnosis
Drill: Every fault identified is immediately followed by a choice: specialist now, or talk it through now.
Proof it worked: No recording where a diagnosis is the last work-related thing said.
Week 2
Bank the rapport
Drill: Personal conversation is closed with a transition into the inspection, and reopened only after the findings are delivered.
Proof it worked: An audible transition sentence on every call.
Week 3
Ask for the five stars
Drill: The review request goes at the end of every visit.
Proof it worked: An audible review ask on every call for the week.
