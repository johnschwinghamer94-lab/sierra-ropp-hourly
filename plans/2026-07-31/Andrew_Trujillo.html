The one-line verdict

The moment on the untitled visit

'I'll run them by my family' is a meeting to book.

What actually happened

After a genuinely excellent walkthrough — compressor startup at 80 amps against a rated 56, a second system at 130 against a 134 red line — he asked well: What do you feel like we should do with at least some of these parts? The customer: I'll run them by my family. Andrew: I'd hate for you to have to do my job for me... if we need to touch base and get everyone on the same page, we can try. Give them a call.

What flips it

He saw the problem exactly right and then made the call optional. Deferral to an absent decision-maker is the most bookable objection there is. Then let's not make you repeat all this — what time are they around tonight? I'll call, or better, I'll get our specialist out when everyone's there so it's one conversation instead of three.

What Andrew did right — real strength, build on it

  • He measures against thresholds and says both numbers. 134 is where we need to be... right now we're at a 130 turns a reading into a fact the customer can hold.
  • He checks in properly and repeatedly. Before I dive in on the second system, what questions do you have for me? is the check-in cadence the rubric asks for, and he does it unprompted.
  • He asks the customer to conclude. What do you feel like we should do with at least some of these parts? is the right question. He just needs to stay in the silence after it.
  • He follows the actual complaint. On GENGA the daughter's room drove the whole investigation, and reconnecting the findings to it is why a two-hour visit converted.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Book the absent decision-maker
Drill: Any deferral to a spouse, parent or family gets a proposed time with everyone present, not an offer to call.
Proof it worked: No recording where I'll run it by them is the end of the conversation.
Week 2
Name the permanent option
Drill: Every temporary fix identified is immediately paired with the permanent alternative and its route.
Proof it worked: A permanent path audible on every call where a band-aid repair is discussed.
Week 3
Close the lesson
Drill: Every teaching sequence ends with a recommendation, not a pause.
Proof it worked: A stated recommendation after every technical walkthrough.
