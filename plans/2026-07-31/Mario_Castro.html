The one-line verdict

The moment on TOLOZA

'I gotta get the other quotes' — and he let them.

What actually happened

A 29-year-old unit, dirty coils, a rusted drain pan, compressor startup pressure at 65 PSI against a 60 limit. Mario taught it beautifully, then: Sounds like replacement is in the boat... It's not fixed. It's not replaceable. The customer left to compare and texted back within minutes: we went with the other company.

What flips it

Getting other quotes is not a no, it is a request for a reason to choose you — and the window is minutes, not days. Reconnect to what you found, then hold the ground with an appointment: Absolutely compare. Before you do, let me get our specialist out so you're comparing like for like — costs you nothing and it's the only quote that's seen your compressor at 65 PSI.

What Mario did right — real strength, build on it

  • The best analogies on the board. Heart, lungs, a mask on your face, blood pressure prone to a stroke — and the causal chain from dirty coil to water damage is world-class teaching.
  • He reframes frustration instead of defending. When the CASTILLO customer said the compressor was just replaced and this is weird, Mario blamed the platform, not the part, and earned I give you guys credit because you take care of your customers.
  • He communicates safety without theatre. Silver to grey to black on a heat exchanger, then you'll start getting lightheaded, headaches, nauseous — concrete, calm, and impossible to ignore.
  • He sets expectations on every single call. Four for four, including the 33-minute one. That consistency is rarer on this board than it should be.
