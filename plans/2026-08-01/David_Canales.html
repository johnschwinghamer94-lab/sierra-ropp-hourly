The one-line verdict

The moment that decides it

He was told he is in a competition and answered with paperwork.

What actually happened

The customer mentioned other contractors coming in an 8-to-10 window the next morning. David's answer stayed procedural — we don't do anything without your approval — accurate, reassuring, and not an answer to what was actually said.

What it needed

A customer who tells you about competing appointments is telling you the decision is live and comparative. That is the moment to name what makes this evaluation different — and, on an ageing system, to get the no-cost specialist number in front of them before the other quotes land.
