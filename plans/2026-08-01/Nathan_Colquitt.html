5

What Nathan did right — real strength, build on it

  • The rundown. Just to give you a rundown of typically how we do no cools — a scared customer was handed a process, and the fear dropped out of the conversation immediately.
  • Permission at every step. Parking, thermostat, gate, re-entry — nothing happened in this house without the homeowner saying yes first.
  • He validated the customer's own diagnosis. The homeowner had climbed up and looked; Nathan confirmed what he saw rather than starting over. That is how trust gets built in ninety seconds.
  • Genuine rapport, quickly. Dogs, the neighbour across the street, the same high school — warm without being a detour.
