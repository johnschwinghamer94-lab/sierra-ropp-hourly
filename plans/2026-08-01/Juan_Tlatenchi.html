The one-line verdict

The recurring moment

He earns the yes and then declines to write it down.

What actually happened

Customer, after a full teach-out: If it's going to cost all that, then we might just replace, like you said. Juan offered — What I can do is I can schedule someone to come out — the customer asked to call ahead instead, and Juan closed the visit with I'll say you didn't accept anything today. On the Toure call the same shape: So I'll let you guys think about it.

What closes it

Both times the work was already done. A customer saying we might just replace is not asking for space — they are asking to be given the next step. Offer a specific slot, hold one beat of silence, and let them move it later if they need to.

What Juan did right — real strength, build on it

  • The best teach-out of the day. He asked permission to teach — Has anyone ever explained to you how the AC system works? — then drew the whole thing.
  • The permission check-in. would you like me to make you aware of it or keep it to myself? earns the right to raise everything he finds later.
  • Numbers with meaning. Every reading came with a manufacturer limit and a consequence, so the customer could see the failure rather than take his word for it.
  • He builds all three options. Immediate, future, and the specialist path — every time, unprompted.
