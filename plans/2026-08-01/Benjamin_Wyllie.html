The one-line verdict

Why it flipped

He found out who actually decides — before he did the work.

What actually happened

Handed a tenant, an absent owner in assisted living, and an uncle with power of attorney, Benjamin mapped it out loud — so this is where David has the power of attorney to make calls for what needs to be done in the home — and aimed the report at David.

What lifts it further

Correct instinct, correct order. The next rung is naming the appointment rather than the report: a decision maker on a phone gets a document; a decision maker on a scheduled call gets asked a question.

What Benjamin did right — real strength, build on it

  • He solved the hardest problem on the call in the first ten minutes. Tenant, trust, assisted-living owner, uncle with power of attorney — mapped and confirmed out loud.
  • He asked the tenure question. Is this a long term home for y'all or a short term? — the question that decides whether a replacement conversation is even honest.
  • He respected what was already done. Contactors and capacitors were already changed; he registered that and moved past it instead of re-selling it.
  • No pricing pressure. He kept exact numbers where they belong and stayed on the evaluation.
6

The three gaps

each one in Benjamin's own calls
Moderate

The decision maker gets a report, not an appointment

Benjamin"I'll give him a call about… do a thorough evaluation of the whole system."
Benjamin"I'll send over all the information to him… see what decision makes best for y'all moving forward."

The fix A report is a thing to read later; an appointment is a decision with a date. I'll have everything to David tonight. What's a good time tomorrow for him and me to get on the phone together — morning or after work? Same effort, a calendar slot instead of an inbox.

Moderate

Nothing was built for the person paying

PatternThe entire technical exchange ran tech-to-tech with the tenant, who is not the decision maker and does not pay for the system.

The fix Build one plain-language picture aimed at David: what failed, what it means for a 20-year-old R-22 system, and what the two roads are. The tenant can follow trade talk; the man on the phone cannot.

Weak

No five-star ask

Close of tapeThe visit moved to the evaluation with no review seeded and none requested.

The fix Seed it at the door, the way Andrew Alonso does: What I need from you is to tell me if I've earned five stars — and if I fall short, tell me first so I can fix it.
