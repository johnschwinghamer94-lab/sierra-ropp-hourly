The one-line verdict

What the day turned on

What actually happened

  • The complete expectations sequence, delivered like a person. whatever you approve, we get started as soon as today. And we do this for a five-star experience. So you can feel if I'm lacking, let me know. Every beat, unprompted, on a routine service call. This should be the recording the team learns from.

  • He gave away a free fix that actually works. Diagnosing the extra cycling to warm air reaching the thermostat, then solving it with closed doors. Nothing builds trust faster than a recommendation that costs nothing.
  • He seeds the five-star as permission to be corrected. you can feel if I'm lacking, let me know. I'll make those corrections. That is a better version of the line than most of the board uses.
  • He asked about time before spending it. are you on any time constraints? It's going to be about an hour each unit… budget in about maybe two hours or so. Honest, specific, respectful.
  • All four critical actions on the closed call. Nobody else on this board managed that on a call that converted.
