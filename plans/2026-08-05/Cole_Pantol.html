5

What Cole did right — real strength, build on it

  • The three-option close, done properly, once. Repair cost, future cost, utility overrun, then the specialist as the third option — and a signature on a zero-dollar authorisation with the advisor arriving inside the hour. That is the whole SILO model in one sequence.
  • “Information is always free.” Four words that answered a membership-cost question and turned it into Oh, okay, then, yeah, send them out. That line should be on every technician's list.
  • Every claim has a rating and a reading beside it. Subcool 0.8 against 8–10, amp draw 123 against 112, a capacitor reading zero where it should read 40 and 5. Nothing is asserted.
  • The best analogies on the board. The compressor's blood pressure, the sealed motor with no dipstick, the damper that closes and never opens again. Customers can repeat all three.
  • He turns down work when it is wrong for the customer. Duct cleaning sent elsewhere with a technical reason, and mismatched piecemeal repairs called what they are: just a band aid.
  • He keeps the pricing conversation in the right place. Ballparks now, real numbers at the end, replacement figures with the specialist — and he says so out loud, which is exactly the process.
  • He quantified the cost of doing nothing. 12% over on utilities, and that's just going to continue to get worse.
