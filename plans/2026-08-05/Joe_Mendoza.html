What Joe did right — real strength, build on it

  • He asks the goal question, out loud, on every call. With today's visit, what's the end goal? What is it that you or him are looking to accomplish? Almost nobody on this board asks it, and it is the reason his presentations always land on something the customer said first.
  • He used a low power bill to make a stronger case. The bill was $40–60 with solar, so instead of stretching for a savings argument he changed the axis: the question at hand is not saving you guys money on the power bill, but reliability. That is Spin It executed properly.
  • He found a customer money in a trade she did not call about. A $325 water bill and a water heater nobody had inspected became a complimentary 65-point plumbing inspection she already owned — on a call where she had just refused a filter change.
  • He solved the problem other workers had refused. But you all won't back my truck out, will you? I mean, other workers have said no, they won't do it. → No, I'll. I'll figure that out for you.
  • He restarted the explanation for the decision-maker. When the homeowner walked in mid-call, he re-presented from the beginning rather than leaving it to be relayed second-hand.
  • He owned a company failure and rebooked it around the customer’s life. Ladder too short, office closed, a technician injured that Sunday — and the customer got a Friday first-thing slot chosen by her: como nosotros somos los que estamos moviendo la cita, yo voy a asegurar que si quieres en la mañana, sea en la mañana.
  • He serves in the customer’s language. Two of these calls run largely in Spanish, and the depth of history he got out of that tenant is a direct result.
