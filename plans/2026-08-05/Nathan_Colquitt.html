The gaps

Moderate

Gap 1 — The expectations frame stops at approval

Nathan"we don't do anything without your approval today, so anything that you want, then we'll make sure that."
What was missingNo commitment to come back with options, no “if you approve it we can take care of it today,” and no five-star seed — on a call where he then found two genuine problems.

The fix The approval line without the options line teaches the customer that nothing is coming. Two more sentences at the door change the whole back half of the visit: Whatever I find, I'll bring it back to you as a list of options and walk you through each one. Nothing happens without your approval — and if you do approve something, I can usually take care of it today. My goal is a five-star experience, so stop me if I'm not getting there. He is already saying half of it.

Moderate

Gap 2 — The bill question was asked in a form the customer could close

Nathan"Within those three years, have you noticed spike in energy?"
Customer"Oh, no, everything was good."
What was never askedWhat the thermostat is set at in summer, what a standard bill looks like at that setting, and which rooms run hotter or colder than the rest.

The fix A yes-or-no question gets a no and the subject is closed. The required version cannot be closed: What do you keep the thermostat at in the summer? … And what does a standard power bill look like for you setting it there? A number in the room is something to link an option to later; “everything was good” is nothing.

Moderate

Gap 3 — The filter was pushed to a future visit

Nathan"If you have any filters on hand, I can change those for you while I'm here. No problem. I'll just put a note on my on there for you. And the next time that we come, I'll make sure that we have some."
Also on tapeHe documented the exact size for her — "You have a 20 by 24 by one" — so the need was identified and priced-out ready.

The fix He did the hard part — identified and sized it — then made it somebody's problem in three months. If it is not on the truck, book the return before leaving: I don't have that size with me today, so let me get it on the schedule this week and I'll swap it then. A note for “next time we come” is not a plan and it is not an option.
