What Robert did right — real strength, build on it

  • A lead off a twenty-two-minute fragment. Previously this day was recorded as nothing to grade. It was a conversion.
  • He asks for the bill and gets a number. It's like 170. It's gone up a lot — the single most useful sentence a customer can say, and he asked the question that produced it.
  • Methodical electrical triage. Noise on shutdown, indoor versus outdoor, lights, television — the right questions in the right order.
  • He fixed a dispatch problem for the customer instead of making her chase it, which is the kind of thing that decides whether someone calls back.
  • Complete expectation sequence on the recording where the welcome is captured.

Three-week training plan

Record the whole call

Proof it worked: Full-length recordings for every call this week.
Week 2
Add the goal question
Drill: One sentence added to a welcome that is otherwise complete.
Proof it worked: Goal question on every recording.
Week 3
Say it back
Drill: Whenever a customer states a bill figure or diagnoses their own system, Robert repeats it back and ties it to what he's about to check.
Proof it worked: Customer's own words referenced on every recording where they appear.
