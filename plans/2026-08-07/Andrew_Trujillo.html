4

What he did right — real strength, build on it

  • He gets customers to diagnose — the hardest behaviour in the rubric. See that darker stain that we got right in there? What do you think that is right there? → Oil, probably. The customer added two repairs immediately afterwards, on his own initiative.
  • He chases warranty money the customer didn't know they had. Called out the American Standard coverage to May 2030 and pushed the landlord to try the installer first. That is trust-building that pays back later.
  • He teaches with the nameplate, not with adjectives. We have a power reading that we need to stay within. And that 45 is tied to the compressor... the lowest that we should see this number is about a 42. Nothing to argue with.
  • He set expectations twice on one job. Tenant and landlord both got the full sequence, including the office informed me that we're going to be waiving the service fee for today.
  • He asks the maintenance goal question and means it. Beyond having the issue identified and seeing what we can do, what expectations do you have of me for today?

6

This week’s drill — word for word

Repair-versus-replace → booked slot

Trigger: the customer compares fixing it to replacing it — in any words at all.

Response, before you pick up another tool:

“That's the exact comparison I want you to have in front of you, and honestly it's not mine to give you — our comfort specialist gets you the real replacement number so you can hold it next to my repair number. Costs nothing, no commitment. I can have him out while I'm still here — want me to make that call?”

Then stop and let him answer. You already earned this with the teaching. All that's left is the ask.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Name Option C out loud
Every call, the phrase no cost, no commitment, comfort specialist gets said at least once, with a day and a time attached. Manager reviews two recordings for the words.
Week 2
Tier the options
Before leaving any home, present concerns in three tiers — fails first, can wait, replacement path — and let the customer pick. Practise on two calls with a manager listening.
Week 3
Seed and collect the five star
Add the five-star line to the expectations sequence he already delivers well, and make the closing ask tied to one specific thing from that visit.
