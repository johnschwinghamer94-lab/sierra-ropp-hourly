This week’s drill — word for word

The thirty-second sequence — for short and repeat calls

Trigger: a returning customer, a short call, or a job you already understand.

Shorten it, don't skip it:

“Quick version of what I'm doing: full evaluation, outside first then the attic. Anything I find, I'll come get you and show you. Nothing happens without your approval, and if you do approve something we can start today. Sound good?”

Thirty seconds. It is the difference between the Perez call and the Kalai call.
