6

This week’s drill — word for word

Deflect the price, book the visit

Trigger: the customer asks what a new system costs, or names where they'd buy one.

Response — keep the deflection, add the date:

“I genuinely don't quote replacements — that's a specialist, because he has to measure the system to give you a real number instead of a guess. Good news is it costs you nothing and there's no commitment. He's out this way tomorrow — morning or afternoon better for you?”

The deflection is already right. The only thing missing is the second half of the sentence.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Finish the deflection
Every replacement-cost question ends with a proposed day and time, not a business card. Manager reviews two recordings and counts booked slots against signals heard.
Week 2
Add the bill question
Setpoint answer is always followed by the power-bill follow-up, on every call. Target: four for four.
Week 3
Collect the five star
He seeds it beautifully and never collects. Closing ask at the door on every call, tied to something specific from that visit.
