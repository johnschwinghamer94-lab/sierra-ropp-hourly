This week’s drill — word for word

Get the decision-maker on the phone

Trigger: the person in the home doesn't know why you were called.

Two minutes, in the first five:

“Sounds like he had something specific in mind when he booked — mind if I give him a quick call? Takes two minutes and it means I'm looking for the right thing instead of guessing.” Then, on the phone: “What were you hoping we'd accomplish today?”

Without it, you evaluate for forty-five minutes against a goal nobody in the house can name.

Three-week training plan

On any call where the person present can't say why you were booked, reach the decision-maker inside five minutes and ask the maintenance question. Manager reviews two recordings.
Week 2
