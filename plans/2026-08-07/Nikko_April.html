This week’s drill — word for word

Two minutes of courtesy, then back to work

Trigger: another company's technician is on site, or a peer conversation starts mid-visit.

Be gracious and be finished:

“Good to meet you, man — hell of a summer. Let me get out of your way and get this one squared away, customer's been waiting on me.”

The courtesy costs two minutes and reads as professional. Twenty minutes reads as the reason the visit ran long.
