This week’s drill — word for word

The check-in is not optional

Trigger: setting expectations, at the point where you ask about findings.

Never offer the version where they don't get told:

“When I find something, I'll come grab you and walk you right out to it so you can see what I'm seeing. Is it easier for you if I text photos as I go, or would you rather I come get you?”

Both answers are a yes. Right now one of his customers is choosing an option that shouldn't exist.
