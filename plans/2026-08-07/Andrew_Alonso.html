This week’s drill — word for word

The budget-signal reflex

Trigger: the customer names a number, a budget, or says they can't afford it right now.

Response, within ten seconds — empathy, question, help:

“Totally fair, and honestly most people aren't planning for this. Can I ask — if this system did quit on you in the middle of the reunion, what's the plan? … That's exactly why I'd rather get you the real number now, at no cost, than have you find it out at 11 at night. Our specialist is out this way — morning or afternoon?”

Then stop talking. The budget objection is the easiest one on the board to convert, because the customer has already told you the thing they are afraid of.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
The objection reflex
Every budget or price statement gets empathy → one curious question → redirect to the no-cost specialist. Manager listens to two calls and counts how many objections got a question back.
Week 2
Hand over the diagnosis
End every check-in with a question the customer answers, not a fact they receive. Target: on every call, the customer says the problem back in their own words at least once.
Week 3
Close the loop
Five-star seed planted in expectations on every call, five-star ask made at the door on every call, tied to one specific thing he did that day.
