The one-line verdict

What the day turned on

What actually happened

Both flips came from the same move — route the money question to the specialist and get them out now: I can get someone out here in the next 30 minutes. Both no-closes that were winnable ended with the same move — I'll give you guys a call in a couple of days.

5

What Joe did right — real strength, build on it

  • He does not discount under pressure. Told the price was too high, he asked a question instead of dropping a number: What did you think something like this was going to cost? That call flipped.
  • Price goes to the specialist — correctly, every time. Pricing-wise, I don't know what new systems cost nowadays… I haven't quoted a system in six years. Honest, in-process, and it produced the same-day appointment.
  • He names the obstacle before the customer does. On Tamblyn, the unspoken worry was the sale of the house: This is most likely going to come up during inspection with the sale of the home. That single sentence moved a stalled pair of homeowners to authorise the work.
  • He wins second-opinion calls without trashing anybody. I'm not defending them by any means, but it's 45 degrees in February — fair to the other company, still credible to the customer, and he kept the evaluation.
  • Seven calls in one 116-degree day and the diagnostic quality never dropped. The last call of the night was as thorough as the first.

The three gaps

each one in Joe's own calls
Weak

The customer re-opened financing — and got a phone call

Customer, unprompted"I wouldn't mind trying to run another credit check… I think we're probably going to try to run another credit check."
Joe"Yeah, take the weekend to think about it. I'll give you guys a call on Monday."

The fix That is not a maybe — that is a customer asking to be sold, on a system with zero refrigerant in it. The person he needs is in the hospital, which sets the appointment, it doesn't cancel it: Let's get the specialist here when she's home — what does the back half of the week look like? He runs the numbers with both of you in the room, and there's no cost either way. Book the day before you leave the driveway.

Weak

Customer, as he leaves"If they can't give me a better deal and I go back to you guys, can I ask for you?"

The fix She asked for him by name on the way out — the trust was already won; only the ask was missing. Two systems down, $815 and $557 immediate, $7,900 and $6,400 behind them: that is the clearest Option C on the whole day. Before you call them, let me get our specialist out here at no cost so you've got our number in hand when you make that call. Worst case, you've got leverage. Never let the comparison happen without Sierra's figure in it.

Weak

He contradicted a customer describing her own house

Customer"When I would flip my breakers over here, they would affect— like it make it stop sometimes. It'll go low. I know it sounds crazy, man."
Joe"I don't think that that's the case… There's no, there's no logical reason."
Customer"I don't care if you believe me or not… And I, I don't like the fact that you said, I don't think that's the case."

The fix He was technically right and it cost him the room. The FSG move is to take the observation and use it: Tell me more about that — when you flipped it, what changed? On a partial-outage call, her observation was actually evidence. Curiosity gets the same diagnosis and keeps the customer on his side.

Weak

Seven calls, no five-star ask

PatternTwo closes, two flips, three waived diagnostic fees, and not one review seeded or requested across 203 minutes of tape.

The fix He already has the perfect opening — he gives the evaluation promise on nearly every call. Add one clause to it: and at the end I'll ask how I did. If it's not five stars, tell me before I leave. On the days he waives the fee, the goodwill is already there to collect.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Week 1
Drill: Signal to slot. Any sentence about financing, credit, saving up, or "getting a price" is answered with a day and a time within ten seconds — never with a callback promise.
Proof it worked: Manager hears two booked appointments come out of buying signals this week.
Week 2
Week 2
Drill: Competitor named = Option C offered, no exceptions. If the customer mentions another company or another quote, Sierra's specialist gets offered before the visit ends.
Proof it worked: Every call this week where another company is named has an Option C offer on the invoice notes.
Week 3
Week 3
Drill: Replace "I don't think that's the case" with "Tell me more about that" — and add the five-star seed to the evaluation promise permanently.
Proof it worked: Review seeded on every call; no customer contradicted before their account is fully heard.

Joe out-diagnoses and out-teaches nearly everyone on this board, and he holds his price when it is challenged — the two hardest things to coach. Two of his three no-closes handed him that sentence and got a callback promise instead of a calendar slot. Fix ninety seconds at the end of the call and this becomes the strongest day on the team.
