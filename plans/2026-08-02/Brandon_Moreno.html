What Brandon did right — real strength, build on it

  • The best analogy on the board. If you run a race, what typically happens when you cross the line? Still running, but it's literally on a breath right now. That is how you make an amp reading mean something.
  • He verified instead of assuming. Two other zones were reportedly fine; he tested them anyway — I know they're working. We just want to verify.
  • The expectations promise is complete and warm. Evaluation, photos, approval, no surprise invoice — everything a nervous homeowner needs before a stranger goes into the attic.
  • He showed rather than told. Manufacturer plate, contactor burn marks, the stuck damper — all photographed and walked through on the phone screen.
  • He found the actual fault. A zone damper motor stuck closed on a three-zone Honeywell system is not an obvious diagnosis, and he got there methodically.
