5

What Brandon did right — real strength, build on it

  • He arrives already knowing the house. I got the notes on this house from 2001… Looks like we originally did the installations here. Instant credibility, zero cost, and almost nobody else does it.
  • Seven questions before the equipment. Repair history, failure time, time constraints, thermostat, current state, comfort distribution, last service. That is the complete list and he asked all of it.
  • He agrees with the objection and then extends it. Yeah, it’s going to nickel and dime you eventually, and they stop making a lot of parts for this type of system. The customer said Sounds great to the specialist a moment later.
  • He documents so the absent decision-maker can see it. Video shot on the spot to send to the daughter who was not in the room.
  • He respects the clock. I’ll keep in mind of the time with you — and he did, on a customer with a 12:30 departure.

$6,200 arrived before anybody asked what she wanted

What was knownBroken capillary line, refrigerant completely gone, permanent compressor damage, and a decision-maker (the daughter) who was not in the room.
BrandonPresented the findings and a $6,200 figure.
Never askedWhether this household wanted to keep the unit running or start looking at replacement.

The fix The reconnecting question costs fifteen seconds and changes what the number means: Before I show you costs — given what I’ve found, are you more interested in getting this one running again, or would you rather look at what replacing it involves? Ask that first and $6,200 is an answer to their question. Ask it after and it is a shock they have to defend against.

Brandon, on what to do"weigh out your options as far as going gun ho as far as repairs on a 10 year old system or weighing in your other options as well."

The fix That sentence puts the work of building the options onto the customer. Name them instead: You told me you want this thing reliable. Two ways to get there: the start kit is doing the heavy lifting right now and I can quote replacing it, or I can have our specialist give you the replacement number at no cost so you know what you’re choosing between. Which one do you want first?

Brandon does the hardest part of this job better than anyone on the board: he asks everything, he reads the history before he knocks, and when a customer pushes back he agrees with them and then makes their own point the reason to act.
