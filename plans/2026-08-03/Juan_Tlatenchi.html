1

The one-line verdict

What the day turned on

He turned a pricing complaint into a reason to buy.

What actually happened

The objection was as direct as they come: I’ll sign it, but I want it broken down by parts and labor… I get repairs all the time in a different home. And they always break it down by parts and labor. Juan did not apologise for the model, he explained who it protects: the repair may take a typical time frame or it can go longer. At the same time, though, it’s still the same price, no matter if it takes us longer… That’s why we don’t break it down in labor time. It’s basically the fee to do the repair itself. Then: Want to just go ahead and do that one? Yeah.

What flips it

The other call had the same quality of work and stopped one step short: Give me like 10 or 15 minutes and I’ll come back with a report. Everything before that was excellent — but a report is not an option, and the customer had nothing to say yes to before the recording ended.

5

What Juan did right — real strength, build on it

  • He reframed a pricing complaint into the customer’s advantage. it’s still the same price, no matter if it takes us longer… It’s basically the fee to do the repair itself. She went from arguing to signing inside a minute.
  • He tests the customer’s own theory before replacing it. The capacitor the customer had already changed got checked properly, then: Got voltage, so not the board — it’s the blower motor. Evidence, not authority.
  • Humility up front buys certainty later. Please don’t listen to me — I don’t know at the start of a call is disarming, and it makes the eventual diagnosis land harder.
  • Every reading comes with its rating. 0.1 against 60, 131 against 137, 7.2 against a 7.1 floor. Customers can see the failure themselves rather than trusting him about it.
  • He hands the customer the evidence. We’ll take pictures of everything and you’ll know all the details — the decision stays theirs, with proof they keep.

A signed repair is the best possible moment to offer the bigger conversation, not a reason to skip it.

Three-week training plan

Week 1

Drill: No report without options first. Before leaving the room to write anything, name the paths out loud and get agreement on what will be on the paperwork.
Proof it worked: Manager hears two named paths before the van on every call this week; no recording ends on ‘I’ll come back with a report.’

Week 2
Drill: Every authorised repair gets the bigger question attached. Whenever a system is mismatched, past its life, or carrying a second fault, the no-cost specialist visit is offered in the same conversation as the signature.
Proof it worked: Every signed repair this week has an Option C offer recorded in the notes alongside it.

Week 3
