The one-line verdict

The pattern in one line

His teaching creates buyers. Twice he served them, twice he argued with them.

What actually happened

On the two calls that flipped, Noah's discovery and teaching did the work — a homeowner told him if you tell me that we need new ones or something like that, that's honestly not the end of the world, we kind of knew something like that was coming, and ServiceTitan shows a lead created off that job the same day. On the two that didn't, a customer said All right. We definitely need that and Noah replied Absolutely. But, I mean, I don't wanna charge you something and be like, hey, this is it, without looking at the whole system — and another asked if we go with the new system, when would it get put in? and got a logistics answer instead of an appointment.

What it means

The front half of Noah's call is producing more buying signals than anyone else's on this board — that is now measurable, not a hunch. The whole coaching job is the reflex at the moment the signal arrives: capture it first, be thorough second. Yes on the ticket, then twenty more minutes of evaluation. Nothing about that order is less honest, and it is the difference between fifty percent and something much higher.

What Noah did right — real strength, build on it

  • Two flips out of four, and the hard one flipped. The homeowner who said I can't afford another one, so I'm trying to hang on to it is on the board as a real lead. That is not luck — it followed an honest answer about mould, a power-bill question she didn't notice being asked, and a coil explanation she could repeat back.
  • The most complete welcome on the board, four for four. Evaluation, permission, report, approval, same-day start and the five-star seed, plus with today's visit, what are you hoping to achieve? every single time.
  • Discovery that produces buying signals unprompted. If you tell me that we need new ones, that's honestly not the end of the world on one call; if we go with the new system, when would it get put in? on another. Other techs have to manufacture these. Noah's questioning generates them.
  • Physical first, photos second. He walks customers to the equipment before showing them anything on a screen, and he says why — a small sequencing choice that makes the whole presentation land harder.
  • Teaching customers thank him for. Locked-rotor amps, compression ratio, temperature split and rust chemistry, all translated into plain English with a comprehension check at the end of each one.

Gap 1 — "We definitely need that" — the yes he argued with

CustomerHow much is that? — Noah: I think it's like 550 bucks. — Customer: All right. We definitely need that.
NoahAbsolutely. But, I mean, I don't wanna charge you something and be like, hey, this is it, without looking at the whole system.
Customer, minutes laterHey, so I just need to get it started. I mean, if you gotta put that part in to get it started. — and again Noah deferred to finishing the evaluation.

The fix The customer said yes twice. The instinct behind the deferral is honourable — Noah doesn't want to charge for a part and leave a broken system — but he expressed it as a reason to wait instead of a reason to be thorough. Reverse the order: "Perfect, I'll get that on the ticket right now. Give me twenty minutes to check the rest so we're not back here in a week — then I'll show you everything in one report." Capture the yes, then earn it.

CustomerYou guys are expensive.
NoahI know, I know we are. But it's done right the first time. Honestly.

The fix This call still produced a lead, so the coaching here is about cost, not outcome — it worked despite these two moments, not because of them. Price pushback is information, not an attack: "Fair. Can I ask what you're comparing it to? I'd rather know what you're weighing than guess." And when a customer has already said money is tight, the past-decisions lecture spends trust that the rest of the call had to earn back.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Take the yes
Drill: Rule for the week: when a customer agrees to anything, it goes on the ticket in that moment. Thoroughness is the sentence that comes after, never the reason to wait. Manager role-plays five we definitely need that scenarios.
Proof it worked: No tape where a stated yes is followed by a deferral.
Week 2
Signal to slot
Drill: Any customer question about replacement, timing or cost of a new system is answered with a proposed day and time for the advisor within ten seconds. Ten reps.
Proof it worked: Every replacement question on tape followed by a specific time offer.
Week 3
Curious, not defensive
Drill: Price objections get a question, never a justification. Ten reps on you guys are expensive and my home warranty covers it. Plus the five-star ask on every completed visit.
Proof it worked: Every objection on tape followed by a question; review asked on all completed visits.
