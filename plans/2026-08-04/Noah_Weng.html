5

What Noah did right — real strength, build on it

  • His expectations line asks permission to be thorough. If I happen to notice anything other than the main issue, do you guys want me to let you know about it? The customer says yes, and the scope of the visit doubles with their consent.
  • The no-surprise-bills promise. I don’t do anything without your guys’ approval, so no unexpected bills. One sentence that removes the main reason customers stay guarded.
  • He teaches better than anyone here. When you go in with the heat to try to seal the leak, you can create other leaks. And in the long run, it can be more of a headache. Even in a call that did not close, that builds trust.
  • He gets customers to expand the scope themselves. On Rizo the customer answered his permission question with Do a whole overhaul. That is the model working exactly as intended.
  • His discovery covers comfort, cost and history every time. Rooms that take longer to cool, summer bill spikes, filter frequency, prior service — nothing gets skipped.
