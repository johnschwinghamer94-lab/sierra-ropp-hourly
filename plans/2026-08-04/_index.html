Bottom line

One pattern runs through every plan on this page. The Welcome and Assessment steps are in good shape across the team — expectations get set, discovery questions get asked, and the diagnosis is usually right. The Decision step is where the day is lost: on the large majority of graded calls no second path was named out loud before the technician went to write the report. The flips that did happen almost all came from the same single sentence — naming the specialist or comfort-advisor visit as a real option in the room. That sentence, said at the end of every diagnosis instead of one in three, is the whole gap.
