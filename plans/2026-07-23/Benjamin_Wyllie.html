This week's drill — the healthy-system ask

When there is nothing wrong and nothing to sell

"Good news — everything's within spec, so there's nothing you need from me today. Two quick things: what does a normal summer bill run you at 76? And I promised you a five-star visit — if I got there, would you mind saying so in a review?"

A clean tune-up is the easiest review of the week and the easiest bill question to ask, because nothing is being sold on the back of either.
