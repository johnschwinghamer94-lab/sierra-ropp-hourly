The one-line verdict

The call that defines the day

Handed an open goal, he told the truth instead.

What he did

An elderly customer, recovering from a bad fall, whose regular technician had died, showed him a competitor's quote and asked if she was being taken advantage of. He read the diagnosis back — capacitor, contactor pitted, fan motor oily, rusty — checked the warranty position, and then refused the easy sale: our prices are kind of similar to these… that's pretty competitive. Then he explained what she was actually paying for: having the backup warranty — if that part goes out within a year, that part is covered at no cost to you. That's the stuff on the back end that's kind of where that price is. And then he waived Sierra's fee.

What he did not do

Book anything. He offered his own evaluation — I can show you what our prices are, but I would have to run the evaluation. So is that something you want me to do? — and offered the maintenance twice, and left with neither. A customer who is comparing two contractors on price and trust had just been given a reason to trust him, and no reason to call back.

6

This week's drill — replace the yes-or-no with a next step

Whenever you would ask "is that something you want me to do?"

"While I'm here, let me run our evaluation so you've got our number alongside theirs — no charge, no obligation. Worst case you've got two quotes instead of one."

Same offer, no exit built into it. He is already the most trusted person in that room; the only thing missing is being on the shortlist.

Bottom line

Handed a chance to undercut a competitor in front of an anxious, recently bereaved customer, Juan read the quote, said it was fair, explained what the money buys, and waived Sierra's own fee. That is the best thing anyone did on this board today. The cost is that he left with nothing — no evaluation, no quote, no appointment and no membership — from a customer who had just told him she has nobody to call. Offer the next step instead of the yes-or-no, and this becomes a win as well as the right thing to do.
