Bottom line

The strongest front half the board has recorded and the weakest back half. Every one of twelve reps was Strong at the door, the required questions are landing more often than they were a week ago, and the teaching — paper clips, blood pressure, the Statue of Liberty, staggered thermostats — is genuinely good. Three reps converted: two Option C handoffs, an advisor dispatched on site, and a membership signed on a call with nothing to fix. But four customers asked to be sold to and none of them were, nine reps have no tape that reaches an option, and thirty calls produced two review requests. Nobody on this board needs to be taught how to earn trust. They need to be taught to ask for something while they still have it.
