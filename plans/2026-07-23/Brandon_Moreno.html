1

The one-line verdict

Why three flipped

He offers a person, a time, and no obligation — in that order.

What actually happened

To a customer already collecting quotes: we basically get a guy that comes out, he gets measurements, he basically gives you that quote… we have somebody available within 30 minutes, followed by no obligation, you know, because you got the other quotes. And on the second-opinion call he waived the fee and asked for the review in the same breath: today, since it was second opinion, there's no evaluation fee. All we ask is as long as the service provided was good, feel free to leave us a review.

Why that works

Three moves, every time: a named person, a real time window, and an explicit no-obligation. Customers already shopping do not need persuading — they need a slot. Brandon gave them one, confirmed it, and told them they would get a text and a call when the specialist was on the way.

What Brandon did right — real strength, build on it

  • Three of four, his best recorded day. Two appointments booked on tape with confirmed windows and text notifications arranged.
  • He asked for the review. First time across four graded days, and he tied it to word of mouth — the exact channel that brought the customer to us.
  • He waived the second-opinion fee and said so. Free evaluation, honest findings, and the appointment followed naturally.
  • He admitted that second opinions sometimes overturn replacements. Saying that to a customer holding competing quotes is why they believed his recommendation.
  • The one-part-in-seventeen framing settles the compressor-versus-replacement question faster than any price comparison.

The fix A customer volunteering that he would recommend us to his warranty company is a customer who wants to use us. "They won't send us — but here's what it costs to just have us instead, and here's what our membership covers." He handed back a warm prospect.

Gap 3 — the diagram deserves a photograph every time

CustomerDo you mind if I get a picture of the diagram? Because I like the diagram that you drew.
What that tells usThe teaching is the most valued part of his visit, and it currently survives only if the customer thinks to photograph it.

The fix Small and free: photograph the diagram yourself and email it with the report. It keeps the explanation in the house after he leaves, which is exactly when the household discussion happens.
