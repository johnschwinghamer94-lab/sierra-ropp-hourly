The one-line verdict

Why it converted

He solved the decision-maker problem instead of waiting for it to solve itself.

What he did

The husband decides, leaves at three in the morning and gets home at seven. Rather than losing the day, he offered three ways in: I'm happy to call back, I'm happy to come back, then I can at least schedule something so that way they can come by to talk with your husband tomorrow, then I can also give you a call later tonight… let your husband know we've scheduled someone. Signature taken: this is just acknowledging that we're scheduling someone to come on out. Again, it'll be a completely free estimate.

What he still does not do

No five-star standard at either door and no review asked for, on a call where he waived the fee, produced a $0 invoice, left his card and promised a callback that night. Third graded day running with no ask.

Strong
The best single explanation of the day, with a video to go with it: this is the system's light switch, turns it on, turns it off… when it calls for cooling, that piece presses in.
