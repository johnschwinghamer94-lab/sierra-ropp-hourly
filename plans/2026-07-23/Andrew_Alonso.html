The one-line verdict

Why it didn't close, and why that is fine

He built the complete case and lost to a warranty, not to a mistake.

What he did

Found an active gas leak mid-maintenance and stopped to chase it for free: I smelled the active gas. I need to go grab some soap bubbles and find out where it's coming from, because that's a safety hazard. I'm not going to charge you to find out where it is. Then priced everything — $251 for the gas stop, $1,123 for the drain pan and line clearing, the heat exchanger somewhere within $5,200 to $7,200, and the downstairs bundle about $9,400 — checked the manufacturer warranty himself and told the customer exactly how long he had: you only have 15 months left in the warranty.

Why the customer waited

I'm gonna wait for it to break down so that warranty will pay for part of it, and I would use you guys. That is a rational decision by an informed customer, not a failed close. Andrew pushed once more, correctly — this exact conversation is going to happen again. You're gonna have to compare replacement versus repair costs when that happens. I would prefer for you to be a little more educated when that day comes — and then let it go.
