The one-line verdict

5

What Cole did right — real strength, build on it

  • A customer phoned the office to commend him mid-visit. That is not a review — that is somebody going out of their way.
  • Six conversions across three graded days. Consistency at this level is not luck.
  • He answers what people actually say. A widower talking about where he will be buried got I hope you're here for a long time, not a pivot back to the thermostat.
  • The opening is word-for-word repeatable, which is exactly why it is fast, complete and never skipped.
  • He takes the odd specific complaint seriously — a possible duct kink, one room that never cools — instead of folding it into a generic inspection.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Record one full call
Drill: Recorder running through the report and presentation, or a manager ride-along, until at least one complete call exists.
Proof it worked: A presentation on tape.
Week 2
Add the bill line
Drill: Power-bill question inserted into the fixed welcome sequence.
Proof it worked: Customer's own figure on every recording.
Week 3
Convert praise into reviews
Drill: Any verbal compliment is answered with a specific request for a public review.
Proof it worked: A review asked for every time praise is offered.
