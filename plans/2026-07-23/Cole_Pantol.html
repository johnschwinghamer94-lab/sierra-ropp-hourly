The one-line verdict

Customers are calling the office to praise him, and no tape has ever shown him closing anything.

What the customers say

A returning customer, before Cole had done anything on this visit: I called the office today and I told them that you and Mario were outstanding… I wanted them to send a recommendation to your boss.
