The one-line verdict

Why it flipped

The third option, named, priced at nothing, and signed for.

What actually happened

He set up the whole comparison honestly — we can start throwing parts at this, it's just, it is going to add up — explained what the advisor actually does, they measure up the system, they take about an hour of your time and they give you a price on what it would be to start over fresh with brand new warranties, admitted plainly that he does not know replacement costs, and then took the zero-dollar authorisation on the spot.

Why that works

Three moves in order: the repair total, the honest limit of his own knowledge, and a free visit that fills the gap. Admitting what he cannot price is what makes the advisor necessary rather than optional — and the customer signed for it without hesitation.
