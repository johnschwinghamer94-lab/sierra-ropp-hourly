The one-line verdict

Why it converted

The specialist was part of the plan from minute one.

What is on tape

He restated the customer's own request back to her, described the sequence — diagnosis first, then a quote from someone who prices systems — and made the second visit sound like the natural half of the first rather than an upsell bolted on at the end.

Why that works

Most Option C conversations happen after the bad news, when the customer is already braced. Naming it in the welcome makes it the shape of the appointment rather than a reaction to it — and it converted on a fifty-three-minute call whose findings never reached tape.
