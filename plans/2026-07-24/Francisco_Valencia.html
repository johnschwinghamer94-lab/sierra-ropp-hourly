1

The one-line verdict

Why nothing converted

The diagnosis is complete and the tape stops before anyone is asked anything.

What is on tape

Precise, honest technical work: a motor start device as the immediate item, a compressor already damaged — you can't open and replace that wiring that it's missing… when it goes out, we have to replace the whole thing — and the customer following it well enough to summarise it back. On another call a home-warranty technician had refused to go on the roof and the customer had been left without cooling for two nights.

What is missing

Every one of these three calls contains a real opening — a failing compressor, a home-warranty company that would not do the job, a two-property maintenance visit with water damage history. None of them reaches an offer on tape, and none produced a lead. This is the fourth graded day where the strongest closer on this board is invisible after the walk-through.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Convert the warranty failure
Drill: Any customer let down by a warranty provider hears the membership and our direct pricing before the visit ends. Ten reps.
Proof it worked: An offer on every warranty-failure call.
Week 2
Route the failing compressor
Drill: Any compressor flagged as near failure triggers a no-cost replacement quote offer with a time.
Proof it worked: An offer on every qualifying call.
Week 3
The bill question
Drill: Power bill asked in a fixed position in the welcome, every call.
Proof it worked: Customer's own figure on every recording.
