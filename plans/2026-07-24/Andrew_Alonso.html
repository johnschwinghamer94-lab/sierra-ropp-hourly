6

The three gaps

each one in Andrew's own calls
Moderate

Gap 1 — the bill number, gathered and unused again

CustomerMy last power bill was 161. It's never exceeded 200 bucks. We keep it between like 78 and 80.
What followedThe evaluation continued; the figure was not mentioned again.

The fix He asks it now, which was the hard part. The next step is saying it back: "You're at one-sixty-one keeping it at seventy-eight — that's what this system costs you to run at a temperature you're compromising on." The number is only worth having if it appears in the argument.

Moderate

Gap 2 — the airflow concern was offered and left hanging

AndrewI'll get some eyes on it to see if there's not something we can do about that if you'd like.
What is not on tapeAny follow-up, quote or appointment for the airflow work.

The fix A conditional offer — if you'd like — puts the work on the customer to chase. "I'll price that airflow fix as part of the report so you can see it alongside everything else." Put it on the page rather than in the air.

Moderate

Gap 3 — the review, still not asked

Andrew, in the welcomeWe're trying to hit a fifth star, so if I fall short of some five star service, let me know.
At the end of a hundred-and-five-minute visitNo review request.

The fix He talks about the company's rating in the opening, which makes the ask at the door completely natural: "I mentioned we're chasing that fifth star — if today earned it, the survey text will come through shortly."

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Quote the number back
Drill: Every bill figure gathered is repeated inside the findings conversation.
Proof it worked: Customer's own figure referenced on every recording.
Week 2
Price the extras
Drill: Any conditional offer is converted into a line on the report.
Proof it worked: No tape where extra work is offered and never priced.
Week 3
Ask for the review
Drill: Review requested at the door on every completed visit.
Proof it worked: Review asked on every call.

What we owe Andrew Alonso

  • Recording coverage. Both flips came off tapes that stop before the presentation.

Bottom line

Andrew's discovery is fixed — the power bill has now been asked on two graded days running, and two of three calls converted. What remains is downstream: the figure is collected and never spoken again, an airflow offer was left as a maybe rather than a line on the report, and a hundred-and-five-minute visit ended without the review he sets up in his own opening. The front half is done. The last mile is repetition, not skill.
