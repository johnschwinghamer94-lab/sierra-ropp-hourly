What went right — real strength, build on it

  • The complete expectations block, in his own words. I gotta build you up multiple options. I prefer you guys to tell me what you want done rather than me to tell you, hey, this is the issue. That is the FSG philosophy stated better than the script states it.
  • He plants the five-star standard as a promise, not a request. if I fall short of some five star service game, let me know.
  • Real expertise, delivered plainly. Live warranty lookup off the serial number, and a clean explanation of the refrigerant change without talking down to a customer who already knew some of it.
  • Rapport that costs him nothing and buys him everything. Same home state, the brother-in-law in the trade, the dogs, the Bulgarian family's first job in Vegas. Nobody on the board opens warmer.

6

This week's drill — same six sentences, every door

Say it before the tools come out

"I'm going to look at the whole system, not just the one thing. It's a thorough evaluation. I'll bring you back everything I find and build you options — I'd rather you tell me what you want done than me tell you. I won't touch anything without your approval, and my goal is to take care of it today. We're chasing that fifth star, so if I fall short at any point, stop me."

He already says all of this. The drill is not learning it — it is saying it on the five-minute call and the tenant call too.
