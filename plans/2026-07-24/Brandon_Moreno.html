1

The one-line verdict

What the tape shows

He built the replacement conversation the way the model says to build it.

What actually happened

Age off the serial, then the refrigerant: este ya tiene casi 20 años la máquina… este lleva un gas se llama R o freon, so con este gas en 2010 ellos ya paró producción. Then the pressure, in the customer's own body: cuando la máquina se corre con los presores altos es como nosotros como el corazón corriendo con la sangre… como presión alta. Then the rusted evaporator, the burned contactor, the discoloured control board and the whitened heat exchanger — each one shown, each one explained.

Then he made it the customer's decision

He asked the question that decides it — ¿cuántos años ustedes van a quedar en esta casa? → pues yo pienso quedarme aquí — and then linked it: Si vas a quedar en esta casa más de 5 años, yo recomendar agarrando el estimado para una nueva máquina y mirando la diferencia. He priced the repair honestly at two to three thousand and up, refused to be a bandage — yo no quiero poner un patch… nos ponemos un bandit, por último día va a caer también — and offered the no-cost specialist visit as the next step.

4

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • He refused the easy shortcut and explained why. The customer asked him to just put a little gas in it. He said no, on the record, for the right reason: si alguien me mira nomás sacando la gas, ya me quita la licencia.
  • He asked the question that decides every replacement conversation. ¿cuántos años ustedes van a quedar en esta casa? — and then built the recommendation on her answer instead of on the equipment.
  • He priced the repair honestly against himself. Naming two to three thousand for a patch he did not believe in is harder than quoting a replacement, and it is why she believed the rest.
  • The teaching is genuinely elite. Serial number, refrigerant law, steel versus copper, blood pressure, the burnt exhaust — six images, all in the customer's language, all in front of the part.
  • The full expectations block on FAIR. Look, thorough evaluation, options, approval, today and five stars, in ninety seconds, before the tools came out.

This week's drill — signal to slot

Practice until it is automatic

Trigger: the customer concedes the system has to be replaced — "se tiene que cambiar", "we'd have to replace it", "it's probably time."

Response, within ten seconds, before any other sentence:

"Perfecto — el especialista no le cuesta nada y solo le trae el número real. ¿Mañana en la mañana o en la tarde?"

Then stop talking and let her pick. Everything Brandon did before that moment on this call was right. The only thing missing was the calendar.
