1

The one-line verdict

Why they flip

He books around the customer's day and prices the payment, not the purchase.

What actually happened

On the no-cool call the family were leaving and coming back at three, so he moved the whole visit: would you guys be able to potentially come back at like 3ish? — Sure, yeah, we can come back at 3ish, and then set the advisor for after that so both could compare the two options and then see the payment programs for both options. He also told the truth about the repair: this unit is not gonna last you guys too much longer, even with the repairs.

Why that works

Two obstacles kill most of these conversations — the money and the calendar — and Francisco dismantles both before asking. He is the only rep on this board who routinely mentions financing, and the only one who reschedules his own visit around the customer's afternoon. By the time he asks, saying yes is the easy option.

What Francisco did right — real strength, build on it

  • Two more leads, both scheduled around the customer's life. Moving his own return visit to three o'clock so the whole family could be present is why the advisor slot was accepted.
  • He is the only rep here who routinely sells the payment. Payment programmes for both the repair and the replacement, offered before anyone had to admit affordability was the issue.
  • He tells the truth about the repair. Not gonna last you guys too much longer, even with the repairs — said to new homeowners who wanted to hear the opposite.
  • He fixed the decision-maker problem in thirty seconds. Housekeeper on site, owner absent — so he set up a direct line to the owner rather than relaying through a third party.
  • He stays human when it costs him time. A family conversation about a child's epilepsy diagnosis got real attention.

Drill: Power bill asked early, then used explicitly when the financing comparison is made. Five reps.
Proof it worked: Customer's own figure referenced in every financing conversation.

Train in the van
Drill: Trainee instruction moves out of the customer's home; the freed time goes to the review ask and the membership conversation.
Proof it worked: No training on tape indoors; review asked on every visit.

What we owe Francisco Valencia

  • Let him teach the payment conversation. Two graded days, four flips, and the differentiator is that he answers how do I afford this before it is asked.
  • An expectation card. His open is the weakest part of the strongest close on the board, and it is pure habit.
  • Structured time for training. He is developing someone during live calls because nobody has given him another slot for it.
  • Confirm the after-three advisor slot held. A verbal time agreed with a family mid-afternoon needs a confirmation call.
Bottom line

Francisco keeps converting for the same two reasons: he removes the money objection with financing before anyone has to raise it, and he moves the appointment to when the customer can actually be there. Two more leads today. What is still missing is the front of the call — no report framing, no goal question, no bill figure — which means his excellent close is doing all the work unaided. Build the open and he will not have to work as hard at the end.
