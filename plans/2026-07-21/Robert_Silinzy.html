5

What Robert did right — real strength, build on it

  • Another lead off a five-minute fragment. Two graded days, two conversions, neither visible on tape.
  • He added the goal question. It was the specific gap flagged on his previous graded day and it is now in the sequence. That is coaching landing.
  • He lets aggrieved customers finish. A man describing a bad install, a leaking ceiling and a charge he couldn't afford got heard rather than managed.
  • He checks whether previous repairs held, which is how the company learns what is actually happening in the field.
  • He notices the small thing. A thermostat set unusually low, asked about rather than assumed — and the answer was that they'd been moving furniture.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Record the whole call
Drill: Recorder confirmed at the door and verified before leaving, every visit; manager spot-checks recording lengths.
Proof it worked: Full-length recordings for every call this week.
Week 2
Answer the ask for help
Drill: Any customer citing cost or hardship hears the membership, the payment programme and the no-cost advisor before the visit ends.
Proof it worked: All three offered on every recording where cost is raised.
Week 3
Bill back in the sequence
Drill: Power-bill question restored to a fixed position in the welcome, every call.
Proof it worked: Customer's own figure on every recording.
