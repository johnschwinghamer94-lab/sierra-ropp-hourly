1

The one-line verdict

Why nothing flipped

He gave a ready buyer everything except a reason to buy from us.

What actually happened

The customer said her systems would be replaced within a year. Nikko advised her well — single stage rather than multi-stage given her eight-year horizon, cheaper brands if she's selling, filtration cabinets on the new system, and a genuine warning about companies that skip permits. Then: if you are getting any other quotes, make sure that you're using our quote as the standard for what they're changing out, and start getting those quotes now so that you have a game plan.

What it should have been

Every word of the advice was correct and useful. The missing sentence is the one that keeps the work here: "Let me get our advisor out at no cost so your Sierra number is current and you're comparing against something real." Sending a ready buyer out to collect quotes without booking ours first is the most expensive omission on this board today.

5

What Nikko did right — real strength, build on it

  • He refused to charge for work he didn't do. Bees still on site, system unchecked, no fee. That integrity is real and customers remember it.
  • The permit-and-inspection warning is a genuinely strong argument — and it is ours to use, not just general consumer advice.
  • He tailors advice to the customer's actual plan. Single stage for an eight-year horizon, cheaper equipment if selling — that is honest consulting, not order-taking.
  • He checks warranty registration unprompted and explains how it transfers, which most techs never mention.
  • He agrees signals with customers before going into attics. Small, considerate, and it keeps households comfortable.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Book ours first
Drill: Any customer planning to replace or collect quotes is offered our no-cost advisor before any comparison advice is given. Ten reps.
Proof it worked: No tape where a replacement plan is discussed without a Sierra appointment offered.
Week 2
Finish the job
Drill: Any visit that could not complete the full system ends with a specific return date agreed on the spot.
Proof it worked: A booked return on every incomplete visit.
Week 3
Bill and options
Drill: Power bill asked every call; a three-option written set on every call including maintenance.
Proof it worked: Bill figure and an option set on every recording.

Bottom line

Nikko gave a customer who had already decided to replace two systems a genuinely excellent tutorial on how to buy — brands, staging, permits, what to check in a competitor's quote — and never offered her a Sierra number. He also washed half a system, said out loud that he should have booked a different day, and didn't book one. The honesty is real and it is the foundation. What he needs is the habit of turning every one of those good instincts into an appointment before he leaves.
