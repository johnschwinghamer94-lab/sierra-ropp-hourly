What Nathan did right — real strength, build on it

  • Rapport that customers remember. Three graded days, three recordings, and every single one shows a customer relaxing into the visit within two minutes.
  • He listens properly. The trading conversation is not small talk — he asks follow-ups and lets the customer teach him something.
  • He states his plan before he disappears. Telling the customer he'll come back and touch base is a small habit that prevents the whole visit feeling opaque.
  • On the one day his close was captured, he asked for the review — the only rep on that board who did.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Recorder discipline
Drill: Recording confirmed running at the door and verified before leaving, every call, with a manager spot-checking lengths.
Proof it worked: Full-length recordings for every visit this week.
Week 2
Discovery before conversation
Drill: Thermostat, filters, comfort problems and power bill asked before any extended social conversation begins.
Proof it worked: All four questions on tape in the first fifteen minutes.
Week 3
Always a report
Drill: A written report with a stated recommendation on every maintenance call, including clean ones.
Proof it worked: A report exists for every call.
