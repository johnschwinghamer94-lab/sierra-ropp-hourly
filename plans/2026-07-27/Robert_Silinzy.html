4

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • He got a signature on a no-cost specialist ticket. the first one is going to be for the equipment specialist. You can see it's no cost to you — then a signature and a Thursday afternoon. That is the flip, done properly.
  • He agreed with the objection instead of fighting it. When the customer said seven thousand dollars on an old system felt wrong, Robert agreed — and used the agreement to open the replacement comparison. Far harder and far better than a rebuttal.
  • He handled grief with dignity. A customer mentioned his wife had died in April; Robert paused rather than talking over it. That is not a technique, and it is the reason customers trust him with big numbers.
  • He was straight about a filter that did not need changing. DEMER: this side is black because it's carbon. That's what it's supposed to look like… you make the decision. He talked himself out of a sale to tell the truth.

6

This week’s drill

The block, every call, before anything else

Trigger: the front door opens.

Response, before a tool comes out of the van:

"I'm Rob with Sierra. I'll do a thorough evaluation of both systems — safety, function, overall condition. If I find anything, would you like me to let you know? From there I'll build a report and a list of options, and no work gets done without your approval. The goal is a five-star visit, so if I'm not hitting that, stop me."

You already say this perfectly on your best calls. The only problem is that it is not automatic yet.
