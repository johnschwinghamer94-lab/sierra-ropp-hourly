4

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • The best expectations line on the board. If I'm not meeting that, you shop hands the customer power and signals total confidence in the service at the same time. Most techs promise five stars; he made it falsifiable.
  • He calmed an anxious customer fast. Three units, one dead, a confused thermostat story — and he had the room settled before he touched a tool.
  • He let the customer diagnose first. Listening to the homeowner's theory before offering his own is the move that makes the eventual finding land.
  • He was explicit about the boundary. any work that needs to be done won't be done without your approval — repeated, not assumed.

6

This week’s drill

Close the loop you opened

Trigger: you are about to leave a call where you promised a five-star experience.

Response, before the paperwork:

"I told you at the start the goal was a five-star visit. Did I hit it? If I did, a quick review is the only thing I get graded on."

You already say the first half better than anyone on this board. Say the second half.

7

3-week training plan

Week 1
Recording integrity
Confirm audio before every knock and check it stays live. Report the count daily — this is the priority.
Week 2
Close the five-star loop
Ask for the review on every call, referencing the promise you already made in the opening.
Week 3
Appointment attempt
Every multi-unit or failed-system call gets a specialist ask with a named day.
