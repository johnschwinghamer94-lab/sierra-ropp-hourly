What Brandon did right — real strength, build on it

  • He measures in front of the customer. Reading actual current against the data tag turns an opinion into a fact the customer can see.
  • The finish-line analogy works. you could think of that as the finish line makes over-spec operation mean something to someone who has never read a data tag.
  • He asks the goal question. what's your guys ultimate goal from the visit for today? gets the customer to define success before he has diagnosed anything.
  • He takes permission early. Asking whether they want to hear about anything out of the ordinary makes every later finding something they asked for.
