4

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • The permission question is a genuinely advanced move. would you like me to share with you that information? gets the customer to invite the findings instead of bracing for them. Very few techs on this board ask it.
  • He set an honest clock. about an hour and a half on a tune-up — no one felt ambushed by the runtime.
  • The finish-line frame. Rated spec as a finish line, then the live meter reading against it, is a clean and repeatable teaching move.
  • He asked what the visit was for. what's your guys' ultimate goal from the visit for today? — the answer, just make sure she don't go out during the summer, was the exact hook for the compressor finding.

This week’s drill

Their goal, your finding, one appointment

Trigger: a maintenance call where you find a component out of spec.

Response, at the check-in — not in the van:

"You told me your goal today was that it doesn't quit on you this summer. This compressor is already past manufacturer spec. Let me get our advisor out at no cost so you know your options before that happens — I've got a Thursday morning."

You are already asking the goal question. Use the answer.
