5

What Francisco did right — real strength, build on it

  • He books from the customer's kitchen. Calling dispatch on speaker and getting a window agreed is the single most effective move anyone made on the day.
  • He protects the appointment. When the 8–10 window clashed with the customer's shift, he moved it rather than losing it.
  • He gets both decision-makers in the room. On TONG he brought the husband in and started the explanation over rather than relying on a relay.
  • He frames the whole system. we don't just look at one thing when we come out here. We look at everything turns a single fault into a real evaluation.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Book it anyway
Drill: Every deferral gets a free specialist appointment offered in parallel, with a date.
Proof it worked: The parallel offer on tape on every call that ends without a decision.
Week 2
Front of every call
Drill: Expectations, approval boundary and five-star standard before touching anything — especially on the short calls.
Proof it worked: The full script on every recording, no exceptions.
Week 3
Ask while it's working
Drill: Request the review at the moment the fix is demonstrated, not on the way out.
Proof it worked: Review request on tape on every completed repair.
