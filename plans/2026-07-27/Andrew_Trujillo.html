4

What went right — real strength, build on it

  • He asked for questions before he needed to. Twice, unprompted, mid-explanation. Most techs on this board check in once at the end, if at all.
  • He found the thing she called about and the thing she did not know about. The clogged condensate line with the rust ring at the brim is a genuine save — the longer we have the system running… it's going to peel over the edge and it's going to start dripping onto your ceiling.
  • He answered a question honestly against his own interest. Asked whether Amana matches Trane: quite frankly, not, in my opinion. That is the answer that earns the next sale.
  • He named the drain line as the first priority. the most immediate concern that I would have would be clearing out those drain lines, because the last thing I want for you is have a water problem. Prioritising for the customer instead of for the ticket.

6

This week’s drill

Repair now, free number later — one sentence, both offers

Trigger: the customer says any version of "I can't afford a new system."

Response, immediately after the reassurance:

"Absolutely — we're fixing it, not replacing it. And separately, let me get our advisor out at no cost so you know the replacement number for whenever that day comes. Nothing to sign, it just means it never surprises you."

You already say the first half. The second half costs nothing and is the whole flip.
