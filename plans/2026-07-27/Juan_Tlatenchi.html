What Juan did right — real strength, build on it

  • He removes the risk from saying yes. It's no charge for him to come out is why the Yanoff authorisation got signed.
  • He books during the call. Not a callback, not a number — a specialist actually dispatched while he was still on site.
  • He gives the customer control. so that way you can talk to them first and then you know what you're up against makes the appointment feel like leverage rather than a commitment.
  • He proves the diagnosis. Pressure readings against target turn a claim into something the customer can check.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Counter the second opinion
Drill: Every request for another opinion gets the free specialist visit offered, with a date, in the same breath.
Proof it worked: The counter-offer on tape every time the objection appears.
Week 2
Say their reason back
Drill: Repeat the customer's own motivation before presenting any option.
Proof it worked: Their words, in his mouth, before the decision step on every call.
Week 3
Ask at the door
Drill: Request the review on the way out of every call that went well.
Proof it worked: Review request on every completed recording.
