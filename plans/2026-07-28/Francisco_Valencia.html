5

What Francisco did right — real strength, build on it

  • He reconstructs what everyone else did. On PERIES he mapped four separate service visits and found the error inside them — that is genuinely rare diagnostic discipline.
  • Language as trust. Switching into Spanish and staying there for a full visit changed the customer's whole experience, and the transaction was frictionless because of it.
  • He respects a no. The filter customer declined an inspection and got excellent service inside that boundary, with no pressure and no sulking.
  • He reads the house. Telling a customer which of their two systems does the work, from the condition of the equipment alone, is credibility you cannot fake.

SUAREZcompressor failure diagnosed — no repair price ever named.

The fix Every diagnostic ends the same way from now on: here is what I found, here is what it costs to fix, and here is the person who can price the alternative. Three-week training plan

Week 1
Script the diagnostics
Drill: Run the full expectation script on diagnostic calls, not just maintenance.
Proof it worked: A scope statement on tape on every call.
Week 2
Name the number
Drill: No diagnostic ends without a price or a specialist appointment offered out loud.
Proof it worked: A price or a booked visit on every diagnostic recording.
Week 3
Question, don't compare
Drill: When a competitor or a friend in the trade comes up, respond with a question about their decision criteria.
Proof it worked: A question on tape every time a competitor is named.
