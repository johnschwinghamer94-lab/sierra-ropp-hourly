The one-line verdict

Why MACEDONIO flipped

Cost stopped being a wall and became a process.

What actually happened

He laid out the R22 problem plainly — es de 2012 y está usando la freon de R... ya en 2010 ya ellos paró producción — showed the compressor pulling above its rating, and when the money worry landed he moved straight to ahorita con la compañía tiene financia promociones and offered to bring the specialist out: yo puedo llamar los palast, ellos ven aquí.

What it means

The customer's answer was unambiguous — estoy 100% interesada, necesito que lo cambie... que me den el financiamiento. He never argued the price down. He changed the question from can I afford this to how do we structure this, and then produced a person to answer it.

5

What Brandon did right — real strength, build on it

  • He turns money into a process. Cost came up and he answered with financing, warranty and a named specialist — the customer went from worried to 100% interesada.
  • He works in the customer's language. The entire technical case on MACEDONIO ran in Spanish without losing any of its precision.
  • His tune-up discovery is a model. Motivation, bill, comfort-versus-energy, and goal — four questions, asked plainly, on a routine promo call.
  • He narrates what the customer will experience. Telling them the system will cut out while he is on the roof is a small thing that prevents a phone call and builds trust.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Always offer something
Drill: End every maintenance call with a named offer, even on a healthy system.
Proof it worked: An offer on tape on every tune-up.
Week 2
Budget before financing
Drill: Ask what monthly payment works before presenting financing options.
Proof it worked: The budget question preceding every financing mention.
Week 3
Ask for the five
Drill: Review ask at the door on every call, especially the enthusiastic ones.
Proof it worked: A review ask audible on every recording.
