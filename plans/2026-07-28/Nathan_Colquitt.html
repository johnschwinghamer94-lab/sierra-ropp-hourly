5

What Nathan did right — real strength, build on it

  • The no-obligation close is the best on the board. You don't have to pick anything, but at least you know all of the information and a zero-dollar signature — the appointment becomes the easy yes.
  • He books to a real day. Thursday after five, tomorrow morning — not someone will call you. Every flip on his day has a time attached.
  • He redirects price perfectly. Naming his own role rather than dodging keeps the customer's trust while the pricing conversation goes where it belongs.
  • He checks understanding instead of assuming it. Asking was there any part that was a little confusing? after a technical explanation is why a customer facing a $7,500 quote ended the call praising him.

Week 2
End every visit
