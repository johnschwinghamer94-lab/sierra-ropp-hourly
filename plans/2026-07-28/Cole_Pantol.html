What Cole did right — real strength, build on it

  • He finds what other technicians missed. On PEREZ he identified debris in the compressor valve and traced it to a previous tech adding refrigerant to a restriction — after multiple companies had guessed.
  • He gets the decision-maker right. On ZARGARYAN he identified tenants immediately and had the property manager on the call rather than pitching people who cannot sign.
  • He teaches without condescension. A recently widowed customer who had never touched the system left knowing where her filter actually lives and what a dirty one looks like.
  • He states price without flinching. Just so you're aware, before I install it, how much it cost — offered unprompted, before the work, not after.

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Never leave without a proposal
Drill: Every diagnostic ends with a price, an option or a booked visit — stated out loud before he leaves the property.
Proof it worked: A proposal audible at the end of every recording.
Week 2
Return to the AC
Drill: After handling any grievance or off-topic issue, explicitly bring the conversation back to the visit's purpose.
Proof it worked: A return-to-topic sentence on tape on every call where a complaint surfaces.
Week 3
Put a day on it
Drill: Every pending decision gets a named callback day and time before he leaves.
Proof it worked: No visit ends with an open-ended follow-up.
