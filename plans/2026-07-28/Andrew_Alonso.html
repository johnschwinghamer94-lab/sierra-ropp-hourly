The one-line verdict

Why both flipped

He measures, then translates.

What actually happened

On the MONTOYA job he tied the readings straight to the symptom — motor at 225°F against a thermal switch that trips between 230 and 250, amps at 78 against a rating of 83. On the HARRIS job he heard See, it's what it does when it gets hot. It shuts down and answered Yeah. It's overheating before he had touched a tool.

What it means

Specific measurement plus a plain-language link to the customer's own complaint is what produced immediate specialist approval on the longer call. That is a repeatable move, not luck — and it is the one thing to protect as his call volume grows.

5

What Andrew did right — real strength, build on it

  • He converts. Two jobs on the recorder, two ServiceTitan leads — one of only three clean sweeps on the board.
  • Measurement to symptom, every time. Motor temperature against the switch trip point, amps against the rating, each one connected out loud to the thing the customer already complained about.
  • He hears the diagnosis in the customer's own sentence. It shuts down when it gets hot became it's overheating in one beat, which is why the customer trusted everything after it.
  • Genuine warmth, fast. The name, the service, the construction work on the house — he found something real in the first two minutes of a ten-minute call.

Gap 1 — The expectation script goes missing on short calls

HARRIS · 10 minpermission to park, permission to enter, straight to the thermostat — no here is what I'll do, here is what happens after on tape.
MONTOYA · 51 minscope set properly.

The fix The script is not a formality on a return visit — it is what stops a ten-minute call from feeling like a drive-by. Same four lines every time: tour, thorough evaluation, report and options, nothing done without approval.

Solid

Gap 2 — Discovery stops at the fault, not at the cost of the fault

The fix Two extra questions on every call: what the bill has been doing, and what they want different by August. On a system that shuts down every hot afternoon, those two answers are the whole replacement conversation.
