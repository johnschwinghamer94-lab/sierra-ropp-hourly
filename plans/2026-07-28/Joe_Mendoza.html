5

What Joe did right — real strength, build on it

  • The two-part structure is the best flip mechanic on the board. Announcing the specialist at the door, before any bad news, means the appointment is agreed before it is needed.
  • He defuses time anxiety with numbers. Forty-five minutes to an hour per system, four to six hours total, and a promise to report back early — that is how you calm a customer who has been burned before.
  • He teaches the failure, not the blame. The nitrogen-brazing explanation made an eight-year-old installation error understandable without anyone in the room feeling accused.
  • He finds the complaint underneath the appointment. The bedroom mini split at 80 degrees was not what he was called for — he surfaced it anyway.
