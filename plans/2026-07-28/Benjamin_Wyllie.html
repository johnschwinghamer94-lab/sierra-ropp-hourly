The one-line verdict

Why LANDEROS flipped

He named the real fix and offered a person, same day.

What actually happened

He came down from the attic with the diagnosis in plain language — the ducting is very old. Duct work out here only lasts about 15 to 20 years — explained why patching one run is the expensive way, and then offered the handoff: I can get one of our guys out here right now to go over that.

What it means

No pressure, no price, no persuasion. A finding, a reason, and a named person available now. His partner's read of the room afterwards was simply He already said that he wants to talk to somebody. That is the whole Option C move, done in under a minute.

5

What Benjamin did right — real strength, build on it

  • The Welcome step is the team standard. Scope, permission to raise concerns, respect for their time, no work without approval, five-star intent — and it does not shorten on a nine-minute call.
  • He asks for the goal. What is your main goal out of maintenance today? is the question that turns a checkbox tune-up into a conversation.
  • He offers a person, not a pitch. On LANDEROS the entire Option C move was one sentence: a real finding, a reason, and a specialist available now.
  • He protects the decision-maker. On the rental he refused to run options past a tenant who cannot sign — I can't really go over it with the person living in the house that has no say over what can be done with the system — and routed to the landlord instead.
