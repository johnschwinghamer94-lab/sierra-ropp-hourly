The one-line verdict

Why COLLINS is the model

Financial hesitation met the specialist path, not a discount.

What actually happened

He set the whole structure at the door — I'll go ahead and do a thorough evaluation. Once I'm done, I'll give you options. I'll write my report, take about 20 minutes. I don't do anything without your approval — then asked the question that framed everything: What has you in the condition to get repair versus replace?

What it means

What Noah did right — real strength, build on it

  • He converts everything. Three jobs on the recorder, three ServiceTitan leads — one of only three clean sweeps on the board.
  • He fixes the small thing first. Resetting a locked thermostat and writing the code on the back of the device buys more goodwill than any pitch.
  • He shows the evidence. Pressure readings, rust, oil, burn marks — the customer sees the failure rather than being told about it.
  • Cost anxiety becomes curiosity. We just want you to have all your options is the right answer to a customer who is worried about money, and it produced a specialist on site within the hour.
