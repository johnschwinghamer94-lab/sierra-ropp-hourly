The one-line verdict

Why it flipped

The expectation script did the work before the competitor showed up.

What actually happened

Before any tools came out he said I want to make sure I'm setting expectations as far as my visit, asked permission to raise anything he found, and closed the loop with the last thing I want to do is just do the repair and then give you a surprise bill. Then the customer volunteered I have somebody else coming today, too. Just another air conditioning company.

What it means

A customer who has already lined up a second opinion is telling you the standard you are being measured against. Andrew had set his standard first, which is why he was still in the running — and why ServiceTitan shows the lead on his ticket.

5

What Andrew did right — real strength, build on it

  • Best expectation script on the board. Scope, permission, report, approval and the no-surprise-bill promise, delivered without sounding recited.
  • He asks what the customer wants from him. Any expectations that you have of me today? is a question almost nobody else asks, and it is what surfaced the heat-pump conversation.
  • Discovery reaches money. He got to solar, contracted kilowatt rates and the July bill without it feeling like a sales question.
  • He wins against a live competitor. The customer had a second company booked for the same day and the lead still landed on his ticket.

The three gaps

Gap 1 — The competitor was named and never worked

Customer, TROUTMANI have somebody else coming today, too. Just another air conditioning company. To get so I have two perspectives on the thing.
AndrewOkay. — then straight on to the next question.

The fix That sentence is an invitation, not a threat. One question turns it around: Good — what are you hoping they tell you that I haven't yet? Now you know exactly what you are being compared on, and you get to answer it first.

Solid

7

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Turn the competitor
Drill: When another company is mentioned, answer with a question about what they hope to hear, before anything else.
Proof it worked: A follow-up question on tape every time a competitor comes up.
Week 2
Signal to slot
Drill: Any mention of replacement, heat pumps or new systems gets converted to a booked specialist visit before the topic changes.
Proof it worked: Every replacement mention ends in a date and time.
Week 3
Close the loop
Drill: Bring the customer's stated goal back verbatim when presenting the report.
Proof it worked: Their own words repeated at the decision step on every call.

Both needed one more question and a date on the calendar.
