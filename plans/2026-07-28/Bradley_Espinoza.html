What Bradley did right — real strength, build on it

  • He handled a chemotherapy patient with real care. He asked how long, he asked what the treatment schedule was, and he built the whole visit around a 10:30 blood draw without making the customer manage him.
  • The expectation script is complete and unrushed. Tour, evaluation, permission, report, approval, five-star — with the invitation to stop him at any point.
  • He asks the money question. Both customers told him their power bill because he asked directly. Very few techs on the board get that on every call.
  • He re-checks the clock. So still doing okay on time, right? midway through a two-hour visit is how you keep a customer relaxed instead of anxious.

Three-week training plan

Week 1
Spend the bill answer
Drill: Every time a customer gives a power-bill number, respond with one sentence connecting it to their equipment and one question offering the specialist.
Proof it worked: Every bill number on tape followed by a reconnect and an offer.
Week 2
Present the report
Drill: No visit ends without the report presented in person, photos shown, options named out loud.
Proof it worked: A presented report audible on every recording.
Week 3
Keep the door open
Drill: When a customer says nothing is broken, agree and still show what you found — framed as no surprises, not as a repair.
Proof it worked: A findings walkthrough on every call, including the ones where nothing is wrong.
