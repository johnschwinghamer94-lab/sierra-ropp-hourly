Strengths

1. He books the specialist to a named date and a two-hour window (Strong on wins)

Every one of Brandon's six handoffs ends with a commitment a customer could put in a diary, and he goes up the chain to get it. Call 3: let me see what he has available. I'm gonna call the— his boss because he actually has the schedule, um, because I want to promise you guys a date, then the window (8 to 10, 10 to 12, 12 to 2, 2 to 4), then both phone numbers, then I'll just reach back out, just confirm it. Call 6, same day: let me give the— his boss a call, and then we'll see, uh, what the time slots are for, like, around, like you said, maybe 5 or so, landing on it's looking between 4 to 7. Call 17: Let me give the guy a call… he booked it for that time spot. Coaching value: this is the primary win of the SILO role executed with more operational rigour than anyone else in the window, and the "call the advisor's manager for a real slot" habit is directly teachable to the rest of the team.

2. He sells the appointment against Sierra's own scheduling weakness (Strong)

Brandon's best booking line is a piece of honesty about his own company. Call 3: And they have 2-hour windows, because I know for us it was like an 8-hour window. I don't know if they told you, like, we're scheduled 8 to 8. It sucks. But for them, it's a 2-hour window. He repeats the contrast on Call 6 (they're only like on a 2-hour window. So instead of waiting around) and on Call 17. Customers who have just given him ninety minutes hear the specialist visit as the easy appointment. Coaching value: he has turned a genuine customer frustration into the reason to say yes, and it costs him nothing in credibility because it is true.

3. He asks what is actually holding the customer back (Strong)

On the calls where the upgrade does not land immediately, Brandon asks the question directly rather than filling silence with more explanation. Call 15: is there anything holding you guys back as far as getting all the information available, as far as, um, like the last option, comparing it and contrasting to Option A and B? Coaching value: the probe is already in his mouth; on Call 15 it surfaced the real reason (we have other plans at the same time too) in one turn, which is exactly what it is for.

4. He documents the call better than the customer expects and says so up front (Strong)

Brandon narrates video on site — Call 5: this is your technician Brandon with Sierra Air Conditioning.
