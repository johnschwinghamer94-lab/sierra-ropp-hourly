Appendix — Method and Data Coverage

  • Close rate and flips: taken from ServiceTitan via tgl_truth, aggregated across every available day of July for this technician (24 leads created off 43 calls run). ServiceTitan overrides the weekly reports’ own tallies in both directions.

  • Week straddling the month boundary: the week ending Jul 5 runs from Jun 29, so its graded-call count includes two June days. This follows the spec rule that a straddling week counts for both months it touches.
  • Price rule: under the locked SILO price rule, no technician is banded down or given a gap for declining to quote or break down replacement pricing.
