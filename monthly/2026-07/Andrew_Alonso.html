Top Recurring Gaps

Gaps that appeared in more than one week of July, merged rather than repeated per week. Each is cited back to the week that recorded it.

1. Recording coverage too thin to coach from

Flagged in both graded weeks. The week ending Jul 19 called the coverage too thin to coach from; the closing week found eleven of eighteen recordings unusable, some of them because they were not recorded. This is the first thing to fix, because it caps how much of the rest can be assessed.

2. The specialist sold as free rather than as the next step

The week ending Jul 26 flagged selling the specialist as free instead of as urgent. Framing the visit by what it costs rather than what it does is a roster-wide pattern and he has it too.

3. Findings left without any option attached

The week ending Jul 19 flagged large multi-system properties handled as a list of repairs; the closing week flagged a maintenance completed on failing parts with no option at all. Both are the same failure — a complete diagnosis that ends without a choice.

4. No review ask

Flagged in both weeks, and the closing week notes the sharper version: he quotes Sierra's rating to customers and then never asks for one.

5. Calls at 208 and 249 minutes

Flagged in the closing week. Two visits ran past three and four hours without a proportionate outcome.

What Stuck

The Deliver step

The week ending Jul 19 banded Deliver at Solid and flagged the absent review ask as a headline gap. One week later Deliver banded Strong on wins and Ready rose from Solid to Strong alongside it. The coaching landed on the closing half of the call faster than for anyone else on the roster.
